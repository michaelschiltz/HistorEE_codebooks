### The negative finding, which is the batch's actual result

**The entity facet does not separate the unilateral from the bilateral

cells. That agreement is thinner than it looks and the rows say so:

- `LP1` and `AP1` are **one datum each**. Harris describes the *commenda* family at
  PDF 11 and introduces the bilateral variant only at 12, where the sole stated
  effect is on profit-splitting; van Doosselaere 66's *socii* sentence is likewise
  family-level. Both cells carry `low` on `societas_maris` against `medium` on
  `commenda`, on the same reasoning the 2026-08-18 `AP3`/`LR1` cells used.
- `CI1` is **two different sentences that agree** — the only independently coded
  cell of the ten. `commenda` rests on van Doosselaere 65 term 1's segregation
  clause ("carrying his own capital segregated from the commenda agreement");
  `societas_maris` on term 1's addition clause, which names the bilateral form in
  terms.
- The other eight are `.NR`/`.NA` and agree vacuously.

The pair separates on `LR6` and `CF1` — both WP1, both about the **individual's**
exposure — and on nothing in WP2. This bears directly on the dissent recorded on
the `societas_maris` type row: Pryor 1977, Luzzatto 1961, 119 and van Doosselaere
65 n.7 hold the two "essentially the same agreement", each reaching that on the
*payout*. The row was kept separate because both datasets measure the *loss*. This
is the first evidence on a third question — entity *structure* — and it finds no
difference. **No comparative claim may treat the pair as two independent cases on
any WP2 component.**

### Two schema problems the batch surfaced and did not repair

**`AP4` is incoherent across the census.** Six forms with no founder are `.NA`
(`bazacle_mill`, `casa_san_giorgio`, `maona_chios`, `maona_corsica`,
`partenrederij`, and now these three). `isqa AP4=0`, high, is the only substantive
`AP4` on a form with no founder, and the *ʿisqa* is a bilateral capital-labour
contract exactly like these three. Either `AP4`'s locator generalises from
`founder` to *the party who supplies the corpus* — in which case `commenda` and
`societas_maris` are `P` (Harris 11: the pool is separate from either party's, but
the traveller's private pool and the *commenda* pool interpenetrate for creditor
purposes, so separation is complete only on the investing side) and `qirad` is `0`
(Ramli 95: the *ṣāḥib al-māl* "sets aside a certain portion of **his** capital") —
or it does not, in which case `isqa AP4` should be `0 → .NA` on the backwards
reading of inapplicability. **Six say one thing and one says the other; it cannot
be both.** Coded `.NA` here so the decision stays open at one edit's cost, and
deliberately not settled inside the batch that motivated it.

**`CI1` may be bundling two questions.** `ortoq_equity CI1=several-accounts` rests
on "capital entrusted per venture and per investor rather than held as a common

single-contributor entrustment shape. With one contributor there is nothing to hold
in common and nothing to hold in several accounts, and the value falls out of
whichever contrast the coder had in view. Candidate conflation in Sereno's sense —
*how many contributors?* against *is the venture stock bounded against private
property?* Raised, not repaired: proposing the split in the same batch as the
coding that motivates it is retrospective selection.

### The risk-pooling facet stops being a near-constant

`LR4` stood at fourteen `0` against two `1` (`asiento_averia`,
`nakai_fictive_household`). The batch adds one to each side, **from unrelated sources
and on a single criterion**: does the form mutualise a *member-specific* hazard, or
divide one common exposure?

- `partenrederij LR4=0`. Fractional joint ownership pools capital into one hull; every
  reder rides the same bottom and nothing idiosyncratic to a member is pooled. Same
  structure and same value as `maona_corsica`, where "the maonesi share one common
  exposure, the island". Two things were excluded deliberately: general average, which
  mutualises across the ship-and-cargo venture rather than across the membership and
  is coded in `loss_mitigation_forms`; and diversification across bottoms, which is a
  fact about a merchant's portfolio, not about the form.
- `begijnhof LR4=1`, `LR5=diversifying` (the second `diversifying` after
  `nakai_fictive_household`). The infirmary and poor table are "a Court Beguinage's
  social safety net", admitting beguines who had become too poor to support themselves
  as permanent charity cases with room, board and medical care for life, in exchange
  for signing over their remaining property (Moran 78). Common fund, member
  contribution, hazard that falls on members at different times, provision contingent
  on the hazard.

**Both are judgements and both are argued in `proposed-of/`.** `partenrederij LR4=0`
was taken over the stricter `.NR` on the `maona_corsica` precedent; `begijnhof
LR4=1` is `medium` because Moran never frames the infirmary as insurance and outside
donations blur how far the fund is the members' own; `LR5=diversifying` is `low`,
and 1578 Mechelen — the magistrate burning the whole beguinage down — is a common
shock against it. **If either is overturned the other should be re-examined, because
one test produced both.**

### Considered and rejected: splitting `begijnhof` at 1585

The standing rule that a type spanning both values of a characteristic must be split
was applied to `voc` three ways earlier this month, and this row looked like a
candidate: 21 of its 22 substantive cells rest on Moran and are therefore
post-1585, while the declared period is 13c–19c. **Rejected for this batch, on three
grounds.**

1. **Two of the three apparent divergences do not hold.** The `LP1` counter-case
   (Voigt 470: an Osnabrück property transaction of 1320 requiring the consent of all
   37 named beguines) is a *Domschwesternhaus* — Moran's own contrast class — and so
   is not evidence about the medieval *court* form. `MG4` would come out `.NR` on both
   rows, since the dual secular-and-ecclesiastical subjection Moran reports at 79 as
   having "always" obtained is matched in Voigt's medieval material by episcopal
   commissions under *Cum de mulieribus* alongside *Schutzprivilegien* from secular
   rulers. Only `TS4` survives, and its medieval value is not established either:
   Voigt 443 says no superior instance was competent in matters of law and form of
   life, which asserts that *nobody* could amend the rules, not that the entity could.
2. **A split would produce one real row and one nearly empty one**, both resting on
   Moran. That is the `mudaraba` situation: coding both would manufacture agreement
   rather than test it. The `voc` split worked because Gelderblom/de Jong/Jonker and
   Dari-Mattiacci et al. supply dated, cell-level evidence on both sides — article 7,
   the 1612 reckoning, the 1623 rejection of directors' personal liability. There is
   no equivalent here.
3. **1585 is where the source base begins, not demonstrably where the institution
   moved.** Moran's article opens in the 1580s because her archives do. Splitting there
   would encode a periodisation drawn from record survival into the matrix, where a
   later reader would take the difference between the two rows for a finding about
   beguinages. That 1585 is also a genuine watershed in the world — Revolt, Trent, the
   1559 episcopal reorganisation taking effect, the bishops taking the right to
   appoint the beguinage priests — is not by itself a reason to split. **The test is
   whether a characteristic moved.**

**Falsification condition, recorded on the type row so this is not a permanent
verdict.** Split when a medieval source gives a cell-level value differing from
Moran's on any of: `TS4` (who held the power to amend the statutes before the bishops
claimed it); `LP1`/`LP2` (whether a medieval court beguinage held property as an
entity — the Ghent foundation charters at Moran 80 n.24 are the lead); or `MG2`
(whether the grand mistress was elected before 1585). Philippen 1918 is the
acquisition that would settle it, with Simons 2001.

## 2026-08-29 (ii) — ai — a reported result falsified by the next form coded

**Discarded.** On 2026-08-29 I reported, from `maona_corsica`, `casa_san_giorgio` and the VOC rows, that `CI4` moves cleanly across them "in chronological order, on substantive values", and called it a gradient in the `transferable-claims` component: 0 → P → 1 across 1378, 1407 and 1623.

| period | form | `CI3` | `CI4` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12c–1946 | `bazacle_mill` | 1 | P |
| 1362–1566 | `maona_chios` | 1 | P |

| 1407–1805 | `casa_san_giorgio` | 1 | P |

Chios sits at San Giorgio's values forty-five years earlier and the Toulouse mill has been there since the twelfth century. **`maona_corsica` is not an early rung; it is a low outlier that happens to be late** — a closed consortium whose quote moved "solo tra i partecipi o loro congiunti" and which ended as the Lomellini's instrument (Petti Balbi 1981, 162, 170). **Retained, as a weaker and better claim.** `CI3=1` with `CI4=P` — alienable, half-depersonalised — is reached by the twelfth century and holds flat for four hundred years across Toulouse, Chios and Genoa. **The methodological point, and it is the reason this entry exists.** The discarded claim was drawn through three points and written up before the fourth was coded. One form killed it. `CHARACTER-CODING.md` tests 4 and 5 say discriminant validity and the diversity budget cannot be settled from the desk; this is that, in miniature and at the author's expense. **Any shape drawn through three or four forms in this census should be treated as a conjecture about what to code next, not as a result.**

### Eight strains, recorded in the rows and NOT repaired

Per `CHARACTER-CODING.md` and the standing rule that a vocabulary is extended in its own commit and never in the batch that motivated it. Each is entered as `.NR` for no-fitting-value in the row where the evidence lives.

**`MB3` — nearest to warranted, and it now fails in two directions at once.** Three rows:

- `ko_gotensama_1843` and `ko_choko_1854`. 小林 puts the question and declines it at 299 (三つの頼母子講が重複して行なわれていた事実は、藩の強制によるものか、あるいは、農民の金融講施行に対する積極性を語るものであろうか) and argues the second side at 308, with the 岡部藩 refusals of 寛政3 as proof that villages could and did refuse a compulsory kō. A levy structure participants were free to decline and did not.
- `ko_meio_1498`, the opposite failure. Nothing imposes membership; but on 三浦's reading membership *was* the qualification for being a villager, and exit cost every 合力 the village gave — 頼母子の衆中はつれてハ、なにことも合力あるましく候ものなり. Formally elective, materially inescapable. This is not "an intermediate degree of compulsion" and must not be fixed with a `P` — the two rows fail the binary in *opposite* directions, which is Sereno's diagnostic exactly: one characteristic is silently asking whether participation was **imposed** and whether it was **escapable**, and the values answer only the first. **Falsification condition before anything is done:** find a form where the two answers come apart the other way — imposed but escapable — since `ko_daikokuya_1848` may already be one, and its `MB3=compulsory`@`high` is the value under strain.

The withheld *list* leaks too: naming "logbook/4 §2026-08-13 (iv)" discloses that a pre-registration exists and roughly what it is about.

The 2026-08-16 repair — splitting `definition` from `exemplar` — was correct and insufficient. It fixed one leak surface and left the mechanism intact.

### The repair

**Remove the material instead of forbidding it.** `scripts/make_blind_bundle.py` builds a working copy with the withheld content physically absent: the `exemplar` column stripped, logbook sections matching withheld dates cut, `CHANGELOG.md` dropped, quarantined vault notes deleted, and — for a re-coding test rather than a first coding — the rows under test removed. It writes a new directory and never mutates the source repos. The manifest of what was removed is written **outside** the bundle.

The coder is then handed a path and no instructions about reading. Nothing needs to be identified, so nothing is disclosed by identifying it, and the brief need not enumerate the withheld set.

### What building it found, which is the part worth recording

**Running the script surfaced three further leak surfaces that eight days of instruction-based protocol never named.** Deleting the quarantined notes was, on its own, *worse than useless*:

1. the MOC hubs went on listing all twelve titles in their `## Notes` lists;
2. surviving notes went on linking to them inline;
3. `graph/graph.{html,dot,json}` embeds every title verbatim, and is generated, so no link check would ever flag it.

The bundle validates clean: 164 notes, all wikilinks resolve.

That is the argument for enforcement by construction in miniature. An instruction cannot be tested; a script can be run, and running it found three leaks in an afternoon.

### Limits, stated so they are not discovered later

- **This removes leakage. It does not create independence.** Same coder, same model: still test–retest, not inter-rater, exactly as §2026-08-13 (iv) recorded.
- **The operator must name every relevant `--withhold-sessions` slug.** The script cannot infer which sessions formed expectations. A slug omitted is a quarantine not applied.
- **Content that legitimately belongs is not scrubbed** — other forms' row notes, `key_source` fields, dates inside codings. Nor should it be: the blind covers the forms under test, not the whole matrix.

- **For a re-coding test the rows must go, and then the coder cannot compute the next `record_id`.** The manifest emits it; supply it explicitly or the first row collides.

### The result: `CF2` and `LR6` separate on substantive values for the first time

The `agent-loss-exposure` group is `{AP3, CF2, LR1, LR6}`, asserted on the argument that for bilateral capital–labour contracts all four record one fact — the agent is a debtor. `asiento_averia` broke it once, and `bazacle_mill` again, but **both of those have `CF2=.NA`**: they are multilateral forms with no capital–labour asymmetry at all, so `CF2` separated from the rest only through a missing token, which `check_dependence.py` correctly rates as weak — "inapplicability is a fact about scope, not evidence that two characteristics vary independently."

`societas_maris` produces a signature that did not previously exist:

```
{AP3: 1, CF2: 0, LR1: limited, LR6: symmetric}   <- societas_maris
{AP3: 1, CF2: 0, LR1: limited, LR6: upside-only} <- commenda, ortoq_equity, qirad
```

**Three of the four members held identical while the fourth moves**, on substantive values, inside a single contract family. `CF2=0` now occurs with `LR6` in both `symmetric` and `upside-only`.

This is the cleanest instance of `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 4 the repo has. The *asiento* separated the group by being a different kind of form — multilateral, no capital-labour asymmetry — which always leaves the reply that the separation is an artefact of scope. This one holds the form type, the tradition, the century, the city, the notaries and the contract's own drafting constant and varies one thing: whether the traveller put in a third. **`LR6` is not determined by `CF2`, and the reason is exactly the reason `LR6` was added** — a call bounded below at zero and a bounded but two-sided exposure are different objects, and `CF2` cannot see the difference because it asks about *the principal's* capital, not the agent's.

Note the direction, because it is the opposite of the *asiento*'s. There, `LR1=limited` was shown to occur with two values of `LR6`. Here, the agent's own capital contribution moves `LR6` while leaving `AP3`, `LR1` and `CF2` untouched. The group has now been separated from two independent directions and should be treated as a reporting convenience, not as one degree of freedom.

### Postscript, same day: the group's collinearity was partly the coding

Written after `AP3` and `LR1` were given an explicit locator and recoded — logbook 1, 2026-08-18 (ii). It bears on the result above and should be read with it.

The `agent-loss-exposure` group was asserted on the argument that `AP3`, `CF2`, `LR1` and `LR6` all record one fact for bilateral capital–labour contracts. `societas_maris` separated `LR6` from the other three on substantive values. The `AP3` repair then separated `AP3` from `CF2` as well — and that second separation is of a different kind, because it did not come from a new form. It came from discovering that `AP3` had been answering `CF2`'s question on four rows, so that the perfect inversion `AP3=1 ↔ CF2=0` was an artefact.

Worth keeping the two apart when this is written up. **One is evidence about the world; the other is evidence about the instrument.** A dependence claim can fail either way, and only the first kind is a finding about institutions. `CHARACTER-CODING.md` §4 says discriminant validity "cannot be settled analytically" and needs cases where the constructs come apart — true, and this shows the corollary: a group that *looks* collinear may be collinear only in the coding, and no number of additional cases will reveal that. It took reading the characteristic's own definition against its notes.

On the corrected data `AP3` has **zero variance** across the bilateral family — every coded agent is unshielded from venture creditors — so within that family it now carries no discriminating information at all. It earns its place from the entity forms, where `asiento_averia` reads `1`, `bazacle_mill` `P` and `nakai_fictive_household` `0`.

### `CF3=bilateral` is a collision waiting to happen

`CF3 principal count` takes `bilateral|multilateral` and means **how many principals**. The commenda literature's "bilateral commenda" — van Doosselaere 2009, 65; Held 2025, 7 — means **both parties invest**, and says nothing about how many they are. Both senses happen to be true of `societas_maris` and the first is true of `commenda`, so the collision is invisible today.

It will not stay invisible. Multilateral commendae are well attested: "a single traveling party could pool together goods from numerous investing parties" (van Doosselaere 2009, 66), and of the 150 contracts for the *Saint Esprit*'s 1248 voyage, 132 were *commendae*, one merchant holding thirteen with eleven different investors (Harris 2007, 12 n.17, after Berlow 1979). Such a row would read `CF3=multilateral` and would still be a **unilateral** commenda in the literature's sense.

No change proposed to the value set — renaming a state is not additive and the values are correct for the question `CF3` asks. What is needed is a sentence in `CF3`'s definition saying which question it asks and which it does not. Flagged rather than done, per the rule about characteristics.

### Two things checked and found not to be findings

- **Whether `societas_maris` separates from `commenda` on `CF2`.** It does not, and that is the point: the traveller bears his own third and none of the investor's two thirds, which is what `CF2` asks about. A coder reading "the traveller bears loss" and coding `CF2=1` would have destroyed the result above.
- **Whether `LR3` should be `P`.** Profit is split equally and loss pro rata to capital, so the traveller takes half the upside and a third of the downside. `LR3` asks whether returns are shared rather than fixed; they are, on both sides. The asymmetry is real and is in the note — and it is the mirror of the *ʿisqa*'s labour-compensation asymmetry, reached **without** a usury constraint, which is evidence that *ribbit* is not what produced that solution.

## 2026-08-18 — ai (what four allocation forms did to the sort key, and what they did to `RB4`)

Blind coding of `commenda_alloc`, `qirad_alloc`, `isqa_alloc`, `societas_maris`; `LM-0527`–`LM-0563`. Four results, in descending order of how much they should change what the census is used for.

### (i) `RB4` has variance for the first time, and a retirement case is withdrawn

`isqa_alloc RB4=0`. Before this batch `RB4 discharge on total loss` read `1` for `sea_loan`, `bottomry`, `respondentia` and `bodemerij_amsterdam` and `.NA` for four others — **one observed value across the whole census**. The `sea_loan LS3` note (`LM-0526`) drew the obvious conclusion: "`RB4` still has one observed value across four maritime forms and its retirement case is correspondingly stronger."

It now reads `1 / 1 / 0 / 1` across the four forms added here. The *ʿisqa* manager owns half the advance as a debt (*pelga milveh*) and is a **paid** bailee on the other half, so the obligation is not extinguished by the loss of the venture (Ackerman-Lieberman 2011, 662–663). **The retirement case is withdrawn.**

This is `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 4 running again and coming out the same way as the *asiento*: discriminant validity "cannot be settled analytically", and one form settled it. Note what did the settling — not a maritime form, not a European one, and not one anybody would have added *in order to* test `RB4`.

Caveat that should travel with the result: `RB4` and `organizational_forms AP3` are perfectly collinear across these three forms (`1/1/0` both ways). They are in different datasets and so in no declared `dependence_group`, but a WP1-level comparison that reaches across both packages would be counting one fact twice. Flagged here because nothing in either checker can see it.

### (ii) `MC1=allocation` doubled and lost its extension

Before: four forms — `sea_loan`, `bottomry`, `respondentia`, `avariz_vakfi_kirkcesme`. Of the three with `RB1`/`RB2` coded, all three read `capital-provider / labour-provider`: the peril moves to the lender, the market stays with the borrower. `allocation` and "the sea-loan family" were extensionally almost the same set, so the sort key could not be distinguished from a name for that family.

After: eight forms, and the four added all read `RB1` and `RB2` **together** — `capital-provider/capital-provider` (*commenda*, *qirāḍ*) or `shared/shared` (*ʿisqa*, *societas maris*). `MC1=allocation` now spans the entire range of the `RB1`/`RB2` contrast and **cannot express it**. It sorts by the *destination* of the peril (named party / third party / membership) and is blind to *which* peril and to whether it is divided.

The independence runs both ways, which is the discriminant-validity point rather than a complaint about `MC1`: `capital-provider/capital-provider` now occurs at `MC1=allocation` (`commenda_alloc`, `qirad_alloc`), at `MC1=spreading` (`bodemerij_amsterdam`) and at `MC1=.NR` (`particular_average`). `MC1` does not determine `RB1`/`RB2` and `RB1`/`RB2` does not determine `MC1`.

**No characteristic is proposed.** `RB1` and `RB2` already do the work; what has changed is only that a claim of the form "these forms share a mechanism" now needs to say *which* level it runs at. Recorded so that nobody reads `MC1=allocation` as a similarity claim.

Second, smaller point about the same value, and it is about the source rather than the data. Harris uses "spreading" in a second, incompatible sense in his own 2007 paper: an investing party who "split his investment among several traveling parties… would serve as a method of risk spreading" (13), and Pryor 1984, 437 describes Alazacie Carcassonne's *commendae* as "widely distributed against the possibility of loss". That is portfolio diversification, not `MC1`'s `spreading` (transfer to an outsider for a premium). The census's sort key is named for a word its principal source uses two ways. `MC1`'s definition already pins the intended sense; no change, but the ambiguity should be named in any prose that cites Harris for the trichotomy.

### (iii) `RB1` and `RB2` are separately articulated only where they diverge — and which one gets the clause depends on the tradition's doctrinal problem

`articulation` came out asymmetric across all four rows and the pattern is not noise.

In the Latin material the **peril** is the articulated one. The Ragusan notarial formula is *dicti solidi sunt ad periculum dicti [N.] maris et gentis clarefactum* (Held 2025, 20) — it names the peril, names the bearer, and says nothing whatever about price movement. The market-loss bearer has to be recovered by inference from the absence of a repayment obligation, so `RB2` is `analyst-imposed`.

In the Islamic material the same thing happens by a different route. Al-Sarakhsī's rule is that "losses incurred in the venture are the responsibility of the investor" (Ramli 2018, 95) — undifferentiated — but the sunnaic warrant he cites, the ḥadīth of al-ʿAbbās b. ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, conditions the capital on the *muḍārib* going "neither on sea, nor down the oasis, nor buy a live animal". Three perils, named. Peril articulated, market inferred, again.

In the rabbinic material it **flips**. Maimonides makes the manager answerable for "half of all **trading** losses" (Ackerman-Lieberman 2011, 663) — the market side is the articulated one — and the peril side is left to bailment law and has to be reconstructed. The reason is doctrinal: *ribbit* forces the Talmudic discussion onto the return on capital, whereas a *periculum* clause and a voyage ḥadīth are about the voyage.

So `articulation` here is recording a property of the **doctrinal pressure on the tradition**, not of the institution. That is a use of the field worth knowing about before anyone treats `articulation` counts as a measure of how explicit a tradition was.

Corroboration that the `RB1`/`RB2` pair is not an analyst's invention imposed on the period: Hoover 1926, 1180–1182 on the Genoese notaries — "the care with which the risk of loss by maritime disaster is differentiated from ordinary business risks, and the attention given by the notaries to the proper incidence of the marine risk upon the different parties to the agreement". And González de Lara 2002, 259 builds her model on exactly two risks, "both navigation and commercial risks".

### (iv) One device, three traditions, and the matrix has nowhere to put it

All three loss-allocation regimes make the capital-provider's assumption of the peril **conditional on the agent staying inside a stipulated route envelope**, and shift the whole peril to the agent on breach:

- Statute of Dubrovnik 1272, VII,50 — if the tractator leaves the Adriatic (*Culfum*) without the commendator's permission, *totum periculum* is his (Held 2025, 12).
- The ḥadīth of al-ʿAbbās in al-Sarakhsī — capital given on condition he not go to sea, "and if the *muḍārib* did so, he had to guarantee the capital due to the risks involved" (Ramli 2018, 97–98).
- Al-Nuwayrī's fourteenth-century formulary — the agent travels "wherever he wishes in Islamic lands **on safe routes**" (Ackerman-Lieberman 2011, 664).
- **Added after acquisition:** the Genoese standard contract. The traveller "swore to abide by certain instructions concerning the use of the money, such as **restrictions on destinations or goods transacted**" (van Doosselaere 2009, 65) — across 6,764 ties. Note that Genoa and the ḥadīth of al-ʿAbbās restrict the *same two things*: where he may go and what he may buy.

Latin statute, Ḥanafī ḥadīth-warrant, Mamlūk formulary and Genoese notarial practice — four corpora, same device. Ackerman-Lieberman reads the Islamic version in modern terms — freedom from liability "could actually encourage him to invest in a suboptimally risky asset", the adverse-selection problem of a payoff bounded below — which is precisely why the clause exists in all three.

**Not a proposed characteristic, and the reason matters.** A "risk-window condition" cell would be `1` for every allocation form in the census including the whole sea-loan family, where the deviation rule is the same fact. Discriminating power is free; this would buy none. What it *is* is evidence that `RB1` is a **conditional** allocation everywhere in this family, so an unqualified `RB1=capital-provider` overstates in every row that carries it. Carried in the notes.

### (v) The historiography collapses unilateral and bilateral commenda on the payout; they do not collapse on the loss

Added after van Doosselaere 2009 was acquired mid-session.

Three authorities say the two are one contract. Pryor 1977; Luzzatto 1961, 119; van Doosselaere 2009, 65 n.7 — "I have adopted Pryor's idea that unilateral and bilateral commenda are essentially the same agreement", the choice between them being "strictly based on the travelers' basic economic level."

**The argument is made on the payout, and on the payout it is airtight.** Unilateral: investor takes ¾ of net, traveller ¼. Bilateral: halves, with the traveller in for a third. Van Doosselaere's arithmetic at 65 n.6 — the bilateral traveller's half is his ¼ labour share on the investor's ⅔ (= 1/6) plus the whole return on his own ⅓ — shows the labour compensation is *identical*. The bilateral form is the unilateral form with the traveller's own capital bolted on.

**On the loss they are not identical, and van Doosselaere says so in the sentence before.** The unilateral investor "bore all liability for loss" and the traveller "bore no capital risk"; in the bilateral "the liability for loss was proportional to the respective initial investments." The traveller's downside is 0 in one and ⅓ in the other.

So the collapse is a **facet-relative** collapse, and the facet on which it holds is the one the literature happens to compute. `loss_mitigation_forms` computes the other one. This is the sharpest vindication so far of splitting the two datasets: a form pair that `organizational_forms` would have every reason to merge — same parties, same duration, same governance, same profit economics — separates cleanly here, on `RB1`/`RB2`.

The rows are kept apart, with `boundary_basis=structural-difference` and the dissent recorded on the type row and in every cell note. **A reader who wants them merged should be told which facet the merger is being computed on.** Note also that this is a second instance of the pattern in (iii): the literature's verdict tracks what its sources make easy to measure, not what the institutions did.

### (vi) A structural difference between the commenda and the qirad that no cell can hold

`commenda_alloc` and `qirad_alloc` return identical values in every coded cell and both type rows carry `boundary_basis=contemporary-terminology`. That claim is about **the loss-allocation characteristics** and must not be widened, because one real contrast is now documented and nothing here holds it.

Van Doosselaere 2009, 68, reading Udovitch 1970, 190–6: in the *qirāḍ* "any proportional division of net proceeds agreed to by the partners was deemed acceptable", the legal manuscripts contemplating splits "from half-and-half to 1/20–19/20", so "the cost of capital among eastern Mediterranean long-distance participants was a function of market conditions and, of course, of one's position in the traders' network." In Genoa "room for bargaining was very limited": 4,860 ties, 1154–1265, across dozens of destinations and sizes from under one Genoese pound to several thousand, and "only a handful of contracts provided a payout different from the customary" split.

**Negotiated against customary.** It is a difference in how the price of capital is formed, not in whether the peril is priced, so `PR1` cannot carry it — `PR1=0` on both, and correctly. Nor should a characteristic be proposed for it: it is a WP2-side question about the cost of capital, this is a WP1 loss census, and `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 3 would ask whose category "bargaining range" is before it asked anything else.

What it does do is qualify a boundary claim, so it is recorded on both type rows and here.

Incidental, and useful: **van Doosselaere is a readable route to Udovitch 1970's pp. 190–6**, which the library does not hold. Not a substitute — the page range is reported, not quoted.

Two things checked and found not to be findings:

- **Whether the profit ratio prices the peril.** It does not track the peril; Held 2025, 19–20 has the Ragusan ratios departing from the statutory 2/3 in the commendator's disfavour, which he reads as an overabundance of capital seeking investment. `PR1=0` stands.
- **Whether `commenda_alloc` and `qirad_alloc` separate on `VF1`.** They do in the data (`documentary` against `.NR`) and they must not be read as doing so. Udovitch 1970 and Khalilieh 2020 are not held with text; the `.NR` is a gap in the library. See logbook 5.

## 2026-08-17 — ai (what the Amsterdam acts say that the matrix cannot hold)

Five things came out of the bodemerij corpus that bear on the instrument rather than on the form. **None of them is a proposal to add a characteristic.** Four are recorded here because the matrix cannot hold them; the fifth is a well-formedness problem in an existing pair, offered for the maintainer's decision and not repaired.

### (i) `RB1` and `RB2` use one role label for two different people

`RB1` is coded `capital-provider` and means the **lender**: he advances the money and loses it if the ship is lost. `RB2` is coded `capital-provider` and means the **borrower**: the opgeld is a fixed figure that does not move with the price the cargo fetches, so the market loss stays with the owner of the goods.

Both codings are right and together they are unreadable. The two characteristics were written for the commenda-shaped case, where one party brings capital and the other brings labour, and in that case `capital-provider` picks out one person. **A secured loan has two capital providers**, and the label silently switches referent between the two cells.

This is Sereno's incomplete character statement: the **locator** is missing. `RB1` and `RB2` name a variable (which loss) and a state (who bears it) but never say *of what* the role is predicated — of the venture, or of the advance. It is not the `LR2`/`LR6` case, because the characteristic is not asking two questions; it is asking one question with an underspecified subject.

**The repair, if one is wanted, is a locator in the definition, not a new characteristic.** Something of the form "roles are predicated of the *venture*, so the party advancing money against a pledge is `third-party` where he has no stake in the outcome beyond his advance" would settle it — and would incidentally force a re-reading of this row's `RB1`, since on that locator most of these lenders are outsiders and the value would become `third-party`, collapsing the distinction from `marine_insurance` that `RB1` currently carries. **That is exactly why the decision is the maintainer's and not mine.** Recorded, not acted on.

### (ii) The premium is paid only if the venture succeeds, and no `CN1` value says so

`CN1` asks when contributions fall due and offers `ex-ante-periodic`, `ex-ante-lump` and `ex-post-assessment`, the last glossed in the vocabulary as the general-average pattern — contribute *after the loss*.

Bodemerij inverts it. The opgeld is fixed before departure and falls due 8 to 16 days **after safe arrival**, and it is not payable at all if the venture is lost, because principal and opgeld are forfeited together. A premium contingent on success is neither ex ante in its timing nor ex post on a loss.

**No row was written for `CN1`.** Forcing `ex-post-assessment` would have told a downstream consumer reading the definition that this behaves like general average, which is the opposite of the truth. This is the difference between marine insurance and bodemerij that the matrix currently cannot see: the underwriter takes his premium whatever happens, the bodemerij lender takes his only out of a venture that came home. Winkelman's Verwer quotation is the contemporary awareness of it — the lender charges roughly double the insurance premium, and Verwer's first reason is that he must reinsure.

### (iii) On deviation the risk stops but the whole opgeld is still owed — and the acts disagree about it

The protests supply what the contracts do not, exactly as expected of dispute records. **Deviation, not shipwreck, is the whole subject of this litigation.** In no. 955 the master sailed on from Danzig to Riga and the lender declares "dat hij geen voirder resicque oft pericule begeert te loopen; alsoe naer recht ende coustume sijne penningen verschenen sijn" — the risk-bearing terminates, the principal accelerates, and the ground given is custom. No. 957 is the same shape with the rate named, 16 per cent, and the same phrase: "gemerct hij door 't veranderen van de voorsz. reyse niet gehouden is eenige voirdere risicque te loopen." No. 959 likewise.

No. 1995 writes the rule into the contract, and writes it hard: on deviation "sal de voorsz. hooftsomme mettet opgelt van dien datelijck verscheenen weesen, zonder dat alsdan den voorn. Hendrick Voet eenich vorder resicque zal loopen", the borrower bound to pay "de voorsz. somme **mette volle bodemerije in 't geheel**". The full risk price, for a risk the lender has stopped running.

**But three later acts write the opposite rule.** In no. 3227 if the master sails outside the contract "sullen evenwel die voorsz. penningen op 't aventuyr van der zee als boven blijven loopende", with the opgeld adjusted afterwards "tot seggen ende discretie van goede luyden hen dies verstaende". Nos 3237 and 3241 do the same, no. 3241 promising the lender "verbeteringe van opgelt" for the extra exposure. No. 2001 goes further and makes the instrument renew itself: on the named deviation the first 17 per cent falls due when the ship passes home waters, "**ende sullen alsdan de hooftpenningen opnieuw weder op bodemerije loopen**" at a fresh 17 per cent for the next leg.

**Three regimes for one contingency, in one city, inside twenty years, all called bodemerij.** This is the sharpest single result of the coding and there is no cell for it: the matrix has no characteristic for what happens when the *conditioning event* of a contingent obligation is altered by the obligor. It is not `RB4`, which asks only about total loss. Recorded here rather than made into a feature — and it is a better argument for Frankot's pluralism operating *within* a jurisdiction than anything the compilations show between jurisdictions.

### (iv) Ship-pledged and goods-pledged bodemerij are one instrument in Amsterdam practice

`check_boundary`'s own docstring names `bottomry` and `respondentia` as the motivating case and says the boundary between them waits on **notarial evidence**. This is notarial evidence, and it does not support a contemporary distinction.

In this corpus the two are drafted by the same two notaries, entered in the same registers, often within days of each other, under the same heading, with the same clause architecture — consideration, "op 't recht aventuyr van der zee", the voyage description closing on "wel heynder maer niet verder te seylen", the repayment interval, the opgeld, the *verbindende* clause. **The only thing that changes is the noun in the pledge clause.** Nos 3232 and 3234 pledge salt and its returns; nos 3227 and 3230 pledge the keel; no. 956 pledges ship and freight together; nos 3236 and 3237 pledge the freight first and the ship second. Nos 3232 and 3233 are the same borrower, the same lender and the same day, and no. 3233 is recorded simply as "in aller vougen ende manieren als vooren verhaelt is".

No Dutch term in the corpus corresponds to *respondentia*. The type row for `respondentia` already records Schuster calling it an English nominal subdivision, and its `boundary_basis` is already `analyst-split`. **This corpus is independent confirmation from practice**, and the maintainer may want to raise that row's `boundary_confidence` from `medium` — a sighted decision, since the codings are withheld from this session.

### (v) A candidate form, deliberately not created: the floating bodemerij

Six acts (nos 962, 1999, 2003, 3229, 3231, 3239) have **no voyage at all**. ### Result discarded: the intertemporal-mitigation reading of the avarız fund

Before the sources were read, a vault note argued that the *avarız* endowment was a **buffer stock** mitigating a **perfectly correlated** peril — that a levy assessed on a quarter under collective liability has ρ→1 across its members, so cross-sectional pooling is unavailable in principle, and the fund must therefore work by accumulating in ordinary years and discharging in levy years. That would have made it mitigation **across time rather than across members**, an axis `MC1` does not have, and it was proposed as the most theoretically interesting thing in the census.

**Two falsification conditions were stated in the note. Both failed the same afternoon, and the framing failed beneath them.**

**Condition one — the buffer reading requires drawdown of principal. It is categorically forbidden.** Küçük 2025, 150: *"Para vakıflarında sarf işleminin kesinlikle elde edilen kâr üzerinden yapılması gerekmektedir. Ana paradan harcama yapılmaması esası benimsenmiştir."* Expenditure must absolutely be from the profit obtained; the principle that none be made from the principal was adopted — enforced by personal liability on the *mütevelli* and even on the officials collecting the *avarız akçesi*. At Kırkçeşme the quarter's needs were met *from the nema*, 942 *kuruş* against 6,280 of untouchable corpus. **There is no buffer. It is an endowment whose income substitutes for taxation.** Coded at `LS1=capped`.

**Condition two — a regularised levy is not a hazard. It was regularised.** Küçük 2025, 147–148: from the late sixteenth century the state, in fiscal crisis, resolved to collect certain taxes continuously and in cash, and the *avârız-ı divâniye* and *tekâlif-i örfiye* were "turned into regularly collected annual taxes."

**The framing was wrong beneath both conditions, and this is the part worth keeping.** The note identified the wrong variable. The peril the fund addresses is not the levy but **which household proves unable to meet its share** — Küçük's own account of the funds' origin is that "difficulties began to be experienced in the process of the populace meeting these newly created taxes", and the stated design is that "persons without the ability to pay" benefit. That varies household by household and is **idiosyncratic**. Cross-sectional mitigation was available all along. The levy is the setting; the incapacity is the hazard. Coded at `HZ1=mixed`, since the fund also relieves earthquake, flood and fire.

**What replaced it, and why the exchange was favourable.** The reading that survives is not about time at all: it is that donor and beneficiary sets are close to **disjoint**, which is the census's third contributor–beneficiary disjunction and meets the falsification condition recorded on 2026-08-12 for `MC1`. **A speculative axis was lost and an evidenced vocabulary defect was gained.** The vault note is rewritten around the corrected argument rather than deleted, on the standing principle that superseded reasoning stays auditable.

**Method note, since this is now the second time in two days.** Both discarded results were produced by reasoning from a search engine's summary before opening a source — the Rouen choir school on 2026-08-12, this on 2026-08-13. In both cases the correction came from the first page of the first PDF. **The falsification conditions worked exactly as intended and should keep being written down; what should change is that speculative notes are not written until at least one source has been read in full.**

## 2026-08-13 (ii) — ai

### Result discarded: the intertemporal-mitigation reading of the avarız fund

Before the sources were read, a vault note argued that the *avarız* endowment was a
**buffer stock** mitigating a **perfectly correlated** peril — that a levy assessed on a
quarter under collective liability has ρ→1 across its members, so cross-sectional pooling
is unavailable in principle, and the fund must therefore work by accumulating in ordinary
years and discharging in levy years. That would have made it mitigation **across time
rather than across members**, an axis `MC1` does not have, and it was proposed as the most
theoretically interesting thing in the census.

**Two falsification conditions were stated in the note. Both failed the same afternoon,
and the framing failed beneath them.**

**Condition one — the buffer reading requires drawdown of principal. It is categorically
forbidden.** Küçük 2025, 150: *"Para vakıflarında sarf işleminin kesinlikle elde edilen kâr
üzerinden yapılması gerekmektedir. Ana paradan harcama yapılmaması esası benimsenmiştir."*
Expenditure must absolutely be from the profit obtained; the principle that none be made
from the principal was adopted — enforced by personal liability on the *mütevelli* and even
on the officials collecting the *avarız akçesi*. At Kırkçeşme the quarter's needs were met
*from the nema*, 942 *kuruş* against 6,280 of untouchable corpus. **There is no buffer. It
is an endowment whose income substitutes for taxation.** Coded at `LS1=capped`.

**Condition two — a regularised levy is not a hazard. It was regularised.** Küçük 2025,
147–148: from the late sixteenth century the state, in fiscal crisis, resolved to collect
certain taxes continuously and in cash, and the *avârız-ı divâniye* and *tekâlif-i örfiye*
were "turned into regularly collected annual taxes."

**The framing was wrong beneath both conditions, and this is the part worth keeping.** The
note identified the wrong variable. The peril the fund addresses is not the levy but
**which household proves unable to meet its share** — Küçük's own account of the funds'
origin is that "difficulties began to be experienced in the process of the populace meeting
these newly created taxes", and the stated design is that "persons without the ability to
pay" benefit. That varies household by household and is **idiosyncratic**. Cross-sectional
mitigation was available all along. The levy is the setting; the incapacity is the hazard.
Coded at `HZ1=mixed`, since the fund also relieves earthquake, flood and fire.

**What replaced it, and why the exchange was favourable.** The reading that survives is not
about time at all: it is that donor and beneficiary sets are close to **disjoint**, which
is the census's third contributor–beneficiary disjunction and meets the falsification
condition recorded on 2026-08-12 for `MC1`. **A speculative axis was lost and an evidenced
vocabulary defect was gained.** The vault note is rewritten around the corrected argument
rather than deleted, on the standing principle that superseded reasoning stays auditable.

**Method note, since this is now the second time in two days.** Both discarded results were
produced by reasoning from a search engine's summary before opening a source — the Rouen
choir school on 2026-08-12, this on 2026-08-13. In both cases the correction came from the
first page of the first PDF. **The falsification conditions worked exactly as intended and
should keep being written down; what should change is that speculative notes are not written
until at least one source has been read in full.**

### Result recorded, not acted on: `PY0` needs `P` and cannot have it

`avariz_vakfi_kirkcesme` outputs a collective good (upkeep and staff of the *ulu cami*,
*medrese* and *imarethane*; the quarter's collective obligations) **and** individual
indemnities (named disaster victims, paupers' funerals, orphans' marriage portions, the
failing household's tax share). That is structural half-presence, which is precisely what
`P` means. `P` is unavailable: it has never been used on a nominal characteristic in this
census, and `PY0`'s `allowed_values` — `collective-good|individual-indemnity|individual-draw`
— do not admit it.

Resolved as `individual-indemnity` at `low`, the confidence field taking strain that belongs
to the value set. **This is a known distortion and should be counted, not repeated silently.**
The general question is whether `P` should be admitted on nominal characteristics at all, or
whether nominal characteristics that need it are under-split — the `LR2`/`LR6` and `VF1`/`VF2`
answer. **Falsification condition: three nominal characteristics carrying a `low` confidence
whose row note says the form takes two values.** One so far.

## 2026-08-12 — ai

### Result discarded: five proposed vocabulary repairs, none of them yet warranted

Over the coding of the tanomoshi-kō, the ortoq, the Chinese and Japanese share
classes and the European guild funds, five characteristics were repeatedly
recorded in row notes as strained, and a vocabulary session was proposed to
repair them. **On checking the counts against the data before acting, the case
collapsed for all five.** The proposal is discarded here rather than deleted,
because the reasoning that produced it will recur.

**The error that prompted the check.** Row notes and one CHANGELOG entry stated
that `MC1` "cannot be set" on five and then six forms, and treated that as a
pattern. `MC1` is `.NR` on **four** forms and they are `.NR` for **three
different reasons**: no peril is at issue (`ko_daikokuya_1848`,
`shenhui_gu_alloc`); the form is the census's declared null case, where a peril
exists and is deliberately not moved (`particular_average`); and a
historiographical dispute is being held open (`warichi_iwade`). Summing those
into a single count manufactured a pattern out of unlike things. Corrected in
the three affected notes and in the CHANGELOG. **A count in a note is a claim and
must be checked like one.**

**`MC1` — not warranted.** The genuine class is two forms. The obvious design is
a parent `MC0 peril at issue` with `MC1 applicable_on MC0`, on the exact pattern
by which `PY0` was added because `PY1` silently assumed distribution. It would
convert two `.NR` cells into `.NA`, which is a real epistemic gain. But two forms
is below any reasonable threshold for a new characteristic, and the diversity
budget is already overdrawn. **Falsification condition: revisit at four or more
forms where no peril is at issue.** Candidates already in the census as parked
type rows are `huf` (flagged as arguably a capital vehicle) and several kō.

**`VF1` — not warranted, and the earlier diagnosis was over-stated.** Row notes
claim that three forms show three different *objects* of verification under a
characteristic naming only *modes*, and call for a mandatory split under Sereno's
well-formedness test. Re-examined: `guild_box_brabant` (visitation) and
`guild_box_dutch` (certificate) verify **the same object** — the fact of
incapacity — by different modes, which is exactly what `VF1` is for.
`guild_relief_england` alone verifies a different object, fault and desert. So
object and mode come apart in **one** case, not three. Campbell & Fiske's point
holds against the proposal as much as for it: discriminant validity cannot be
settled analytically and one case does not settle it. **Falsification condition:
a second form that verifies fault rather than loss — `friendly_society_england`
is the likely one, since Prom's sick visitors police conduct as much as
condition.** Read it before touching `VF1`.

*Update, same day, after coding Allen 2022.* `craft_pension_edinburgh` verifies
**need** — the petitioner's represented circumstances — which is a third object
alongside the fact of the loss, its documentation, and fault. So object and mode
now come apart in **two** forms rather than one, and the general condition above
is met. The specific condition is not: neither new case verifies fault, so
`guild_relief_england` still stands alone on that. Position unchanged, but the
margin is thinner. If Prom yields a second fault case the split should proceed,
and the design to test is `VF1` for mode against a new characteristic for object,
with the object characteristic `applicable_on PY1` — since where the trigger is
rotation or a life event there is nothing to establish.

*Second update, same day, after coding Prom 2010.* **The condition is met and the
result is no longer discarded.** `friendly_society_england` supplies what was
missing. Its sick visitor is *"the fellow member sent round to offer moral support
for the ill member, or to investigate suspected fraud"* — the census's first
`communal-attestation`, and his object is **dual**: the condition, and the
claimant's conduct, since lodges banned drinking, being outdoors and working while
on the box. So mode and object come apart **within one form**, not merely across
forms, which is the strongest version of the Campbell & Fiske test. Across the
five forms carrying a substantive `VF1` the pairing is now:

| form | mode | object |
|---|---|---|
| `guild_box_brabant` | official | fact of incapacity |
| `guild_box_dutch` | documentary | fact of incapacity |
| `guild_relief_england` | official | fault and desert |
| `craft_pension_edinburgh` | official | need |
| `friendly_society_england` | communal | condition **and** conduct |

One mode takes three objects; one object takes three modes. They are not one
variable. **The split should now proceed, as its own commit, on the design stated
above.** Two things to settle when it does: whether the object characteristic
admits multiple values or needs a `mixed` state, since the friendly-society case
verifies two objects at once; and whether `averia_pool` and `general_average`,
both `official-adjudication` and neither re-examined since, need their objects
coded from source rather than assumed.

*Executed, 2026-08-13.* `VF2 verification object` added; `loss_mitigation_forms`
0.2.6 → 0.3.0. The two open questions were settled as follows.

**A `mixed` state, not multiple values per cell.** Two forms verify more than one
object — `guild_relief_england` (need and fault) and `friendly_society_england`
(condition and conduct). Allowing a cell to hold a list would break the one-value
convention every other characteristic follows and would defeat the
`allowed_values` sweep in `check_vocabularies.py`, which is the only enforcement
this repository has over conditional constraints. `mixed` follows the precedent
of `HZ1` and `bazacle_mill FP1`. **The cost is real and should be stated: `mixed`
loses which two objects, and the prose note carries what the cell cannot.** If
enough forms turn out to be `mixed`, the honest next step is not a longer value
list but a second split — a ternary per object — which is the design to revisit at,
say, four `mixed` forms.

**`averia_pool` and `general_average` left `.NR`, not assumed.** Both carry
`VF1=official-adjudication` coded before the split and neither was re-read. The
temptation to write `loss-occurrence` for general average is strong and was
resisted: a jettison either happened or it did not, but whether the *general
average adjustment* verifies the jettison, the necessity of it, or the master's
good faith is exactly the kind of question this split exists to make askable, and
guessing it would waste the instrument on its first use. **Owed: a return to
Harris 2023 and Hierro Anibarro 2005 for those two cells.**

**Enforcement registered and tested.** `applicability_on VF1` alone does nothing —
the gate is `BLOCKING` in `check_dependence.py`, a hardcoded table, so the entry
`("VF2","VF1"): {"none"}` had to be added there. Verified by injection: setting
`guild_box_brabant VF2=.NA` against a substantive `VF1` raises `SUSPECT` and a
non-zero violation count. **Note for anyone adding a characteristic with declared
applicability: the vocabulary column is documentation, `BLOCKING` is the check.**

**`HZ2` — not warranted.** One instance (`guild_box_dutch`, endogenous claiming
versus adversarial hazard), four forms coded in total. Record and wait.

**`MB2` — not warranted.** Closed-by-qualification versus numerically-capped is a
real distinction and `guild_box_brabant` states it explicitly, but one form shows
it. Note that a repair here would touch every kō row.

**`CN2` — not warranted, but nearest to warranted.** Five forms cannot express
their contribution structure: `ko_daikokuya_1848` (高割 plus 家割, where the
regressivity lives in the interaction), the two Mitarai kō and
`ko_hokinosawa_1820` (state-dependent rates, pre-draw against post-draw),
`guild_box_brabant` and `guild_box_dutch` (flat within a benefit class, classes
differing). Two distinct problems are involved — a two-part tariff and a
state-dependent rate — and lumping them would repeat the error corrected above.
**Falsification condition: decide first whether those are one problem or two.**

**What was done instead.** Nothing to any vocabulary. The strains stay recorded
in the rows where the evidence for them lives, and each now carries a stated
condition under which it would become actionable. This is the `add forms, not
features` default operating as intended: five repairs proposed from the desk,
none surviving contact with the counts.

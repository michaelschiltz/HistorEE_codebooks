## 2026-08-18 (ii) — ai — the `locator` column, and what fixing `AP3` did to the `agent-loss-exposure` group

The defect diagnosed in the entry below is repaired. Decision by MS: add a locator column and recode; `.NR` wherever the source reaches only the principal's claim.

### The column

`locator` appended to `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv` (32 rows) and `vocabularies/loss_mitigation_characteristic.csv` (26 rows). Seven values, deliberately few:

`entity` · `members` · `capital-party` · `labour-party` · `decision-maker` · `founder` · `arrangement`

This is Sereno's decomposition — locator, variable, variable qualifier, state — with the first element supplied. The repo has always recorded the variable and the states. **Nothing about a value was interpretable across a two-sided form without it**, and the proof is that four rows had been coded against three different implicit locators.

Additive to the vocabularies, not to the characteristic set, so no diversity budget is spent. All three checkers read these files by `DictReader` key except `check_vocabularies.py`'s `row[0]` for the code, so appending is safe; `build_codebook.py` does not read them at all.

**A locator can be conditional**, and `AP3`/`LR1` are: `labour-party` where `CF1` is `1` or `P`, and `members` where `CF1` is `0`, `.NA` or absent, since there is then no labour-supplying party and members are alike. That is the same shape as `applicability_on` and is stated in both definitions. `asiento_averia`, `bazacle_mill`, `joint_stock` and `waqf_khayri` are untouched for that reason.

### What `AP3` and `LR1` actually ask, now that they say so

Both were restricted to the **venture's third-party creditors**. Whether the agent bears loss of the *principal's* capital is `CF2`'s question. The two coincide only where the principal is a **creditor** rather than a residual claimant — the *ʿisqa*'s investor holds half the advance as a debt, the `ortoq_loan` principal is a lender — so those rows keep their values on a principled ground rather than by exception. Where the principal's claim is residual, the questions come apart:

|-------------------------------------------------|------------|--------------|------------|------------------------------|
| `commenda`                                      | 1          | **0** high   | limited    | **unlimited-several** high   |
| `societas_maris`                                | 1          | **0** medium | limited    | **unlimited-several** medium |

`commenda` moves on Harris 2007, 11 — the commenda's pool "was reachable to creditors of the commenda **and to creditors of the traveling party**. So was the traveling party's private pool of assets. But the investing party's private pool of assets was not" — and 9, "sole liability toward third parties." `qirad` and `ortoq_equity` go to `.NR` because nothing held addresses whether a third party who dealt with the agent could reach his estate. **Not `0`: silence is not an observed absence.**

### The result, and it is the reason the repair was worth doing

**`AP3` has zero variance across the bilateral capital–labour family.** `commenda`, `isqa`, `nakai_fictive_household`, `ortoq_loan` and `societas_maris` all read `0`; `qirad` and `ortoq_equity` are `.NR`. On its own question, no coded agent in this dataset is shielded from the people he traded with. **Every apparent discrimination `AP3` was making within the family belonged to `CF2`.**

And so:

```
before   AP3=1 <-> CF2=0  and  AP3=0 <-> CF2=1     across the whole family — perfect inversion
after    AP3=0 occurs with CF2 in ['0', '1', 'P']  — AP3 does not determine CF2
```

The `agent-loss-exposure` group was asserted on the argument that for bilateral capital–labour contracts `AP3`, `CF2`, `LR1` and `LR6` all record one fact: the agent is a debtor. **That appearance was substantially an artefact of coding two questions into one column.** The group now separates on `AP3`/`CF2`, on `AP3`/`LR1`, on `AP3`/`LR6` and on `LR1`/`LR6`, all on substantive values. It should be treated as a reporting convenience and not as one degree of freedom, and the `CHARACTER-CODING.md` §4 worked example — which uses this group — needs its wording revisited.

Two things the repair did **not** destroy, which is a check on it rather than a boast. This morning's `societas_maris` result stands: the pair still separates on `LR6` and `CF1` and agrees on the other nine, `AP3` and `LR1` having moved together. And the *ʿisqa*'s discriminating cell stands, now carried explicitly by `CF2=1` where before it was counted twice.

### The option not taken, recorded because it is probably the better long-run answer

`RB1` and `RB2` in `loss_mitigation_forms` do not have this problem, and the reason is instructive: their **value sets name the party** — `capital-provider | labour-provider | third-party | shared | all-stakeholders` — so there is nothing for a locator to disambiguate. `AP3` and `LR1` name only states. ## 2026-08-18 — ai — `AP3` may be asking a question the commenda answers twice, and `check_dependence.py` under-reports

Two architecture notes falling out of the `societas_maris` coding (`OF-0283`–`OF-0293`).

### `AP3` and `LR1` are coded as though owner shielding were symmetric, and in the commenda it is not

`AP3 owner shielding limited liability` asks whether "members' personal assets [are] shielded from entity creditors" — one value for the form. The *commenda* has two members and the answer differs between them. Harris 2007, 11, putting it in Hansmann–Kraakman–Squire terms:

> The *commenda*'s pool of assets was reachable to creditors of the *commenda* **and to creditors of the traveling party**. So was the traveling party's private pool of assets. But the investing party's private pool of assets was not. … This created an **asymmetric owners' shielding** and an asymmetric entity shielding.

And at 9: "the traveling party would bear **sole liability toward third parties**."

`commenda AP3=1` and `LR1=limited` read the arrangement from the traveller's *capital-loss* side — he contributes nothing and can lose nothing — which is true and is a different question from the one `AP3` asks. On `AP3`'s own wording the investor is shielded and the traveller is not, and `P` is the value for a half-present structural state.

**Not changed here, and the reason is procedural rather than doubt.** `societas_maris` was added in this batch specifically to test whether the pair separates; recoding `commenda AP3` in the samebatch would confound the new form with a re-reading of the old row, and the pair would appear to agree or disagree for reasons no reader could disentangle afterwards. `societas_maris AP3` is therefore coded `1` to match, at `medium`, with the objection in the cell note. **If the maintainer accepts the re-reading, both rows move to `P` together and the pair still does not separate on this cell.** `qirad` and `ortoq_equity` should be looked at at the same time; both are `AP3=1` on the same capital-loss reading.

There is a wider version of the question that is worth asking once and not repeatedly: **several characteristics here presuppose that a form's members are alike enough for one value to describe them all**, and every capital–labour contract in this dataset breaks that presupposition. `AP3`, `LR1` and `LR2` all take a single value for a two-sided arrangement. The dataset has so far absorbed this by convention — code the agent's side — and the convention is nowhere written down. It should be, in the characteristic definitions, or the values are uninterpretable across the entity forms and the contract forms alike. This is Sereno's incomplete-character-statement problem (`CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 1) with the *locator* left implicit rather than the variable.

### `check_dependence.py` prints the weakest separation for a pair and hides the strongest

keeps a `seen` set and prints only the first separation it finds, so where a pair separates both via a missing token and on substantive values, the `[weak: via .NA]` line is what gets printed.

This batch is the case that exposes it. `societas_maris` produces the group signature `{AP3:1, CF2:0, LR1:limited, LR6:symmetric}`, which did not previously exist, and it is the first time `CF2` and `LR6` separate on **substantive** values:

```
CF2=0 -> ['symmetric', 'upside-only']
        upside-only: commenda, ortoq_equity, qirad
        symmetric:   societas_maris
```

The script's output for that pair still reads `CF2=.NA occurs with LR6 in ['.NA', 'symmetric'] … [weak: via .NA]`, because `.NA` was found first. A reader trusting the printout would conclude the separation is still weak when it has just become strong — which is the opposite of what the group report exists to tell them.

**Suggested fix, small:** print every separation for a pair rather than the first, or sort so that strong separations print before weak ones and mark the pair by its strongest. Not made here; it is a change to a checker in the same batch as the data that exposes it, and those should be separate commits.

## 2026-08-16 — ai — MC1 does not partition, and cuts across a line of descent

`MC1` presents `allocation`, `spreading` and `pooling` as coordinate mechanisms. The sea loan satisfies the first two simultaneously: the lender is named by contract (allocation) and is also a party with no operational stake paid an ex-ante premium for carrying the peril (spreading). No reading settles it, and the row takes different values at its two ends — Roman sources make the lender a venture stakeholder, early-modern Portuguese lenders lay their exposure off through separate marine insurance.

Lohsse 2016 supplies the reason. Insurance *separated out of* the sea loan in medieval legal science under the usury prohibition — *"die Versicherung … hat sich unter dem Einfluss des Zinsverbots aus dem Seedarlehen abgespalten"* — the *Trennung von Kredit- und Versicherungszweck* being a canonist achievement of the 13th–14th centuries. Two of the three values are therefore related **by descent**, not by coordination, and the census's sort key records a parent and its descendant as alternatives.

Evidence now from five corpora: Roman law (Pontoriero, Benincasa), Palermo 1286–1350 (Zeno), Genoa 12–13c (Hoover, de Roover), early-modern Portugal (Costa & Albuquerque), Genoa 1564–71 (Iodice, whose title asks the same question). Not repaired here; recorded as an open architectural problem. See the vault note [[Insurance is a split-off from the sea loan not a coordinate mechanism]].

## 2026-08-13 — ai (proposed by MS)

Three provenance columns added to both censuses: `source_read`, `reviewed_by`, `review_status`. Schema change, so `loss_mitigation_forms` → 0.6.0 and `organizational_forms` → 0.8.0. Full rationale in the CHANGELOG; three architectural points belong here.

**Why three columns and not one.** MS proposed a single `reviewing PI` field. It would have bundled three facts that came apart on the day it was proposed. *Did the coder read the source?* — the van Leeuwen failure, where twenty-two cells rested on a source nobody had opened, and which has nothing to do with review. *Has a human checked the cell?* — the sign-off. *Could that human check it?* — the twenty-three Turkish rows of `avariz_vakfi_kirkcesme`, which MS can assess for coherence but cannot verify against Küçük, since Turkish lies outside his working languages. One column can record two of those states and not the third, and the third is not an edge case: it is the standing condition of every source in a language the reviewer lacks, which in a project spanning Japanese, Dutch, German, French, Turkish and Chinese will be a large minority of the census. Hence `review_status` distinguishing **`source-checked`** from **`coding-checked`**.

**Why the defaults are substantive rather than `.NR`.** Two reasons, and the second is the architectural one. Mechanically, `missingValues` is declared schema-wide as `['', '.NR', '.IL', '.NA']`, so a `.NR` default would be parsed as missing and the enum would never fire. Substantively, **`.NR` asserts that a source does not record something — a claim about the world. These three fields make claims about the project.** Overloading the missingness vocabulary with process metadata would corrupt the one convention this repository has held to without exception, and would make `.NR` ambiguous between "the seventeenth century did not write this down" and "we have not got round to it". `.NA` is the single permitted overlap, used only where a row genuinely has no source and the question therefore does not arise.

**Why `source_read` was backfilled conservatively.** 289 rows in `loss_mitigation_forms` and **all 260 substantive rows in `organizational_forms`** are `unknown`. Only the eight forms whose sources were demonstrably opened on 2026-08-12 and 2026-08-13 carry `partial`, and nothing carries `full`. The temptation was to reconstruct earlier sessions from memory and mark them read. **That would have been a guess entered as provenance, which is exactly the failure the column exists to detect**, and it would have been unfalsifiable in a way the original error at least was not. `unknown` is a true statement about the record; `full` would have been a claim about my own past behaviour that nothing in the repository could check.

### Same day, later: the language rule withdrawn, and why the column survives it

MS reports that the Synergy team will include a PI reading Turkish and Arabic. Two consequences, and the second matters more than the first.

**The confidence cap on the Turkish batch is withdrawn, because it was wrong in principle and not merely superseded.** The rule agreed this morning was that no cell resting solely on a Turkish source could exceed `medium`, since MS could not audit it. That **conflates evidential strength with reviewer availability.** `confidence` records how well the source supports the cell; whether anyone has checked it is a fact about the project, and it now has its own fields. A figure quoted unambiguously from an account book is not weaker evidence because the reviewer lacks the language. Adding `review_status` is precisely what makes the conflation visible — before today there was nowhere else for that information to go, which is how it ended up distorting `confidence`. **General rule: when a project fact starts leaking into an evidential field, the answer is a field, not a convention.** The existing confidences on those rows are left standing, having in practice been set on evidential grounds: `RB3=low` is a source-critical judgement about scribal formulae and `GV1=.NR` is Küçük's silence, neither a language penalty.

**`coding-checked` is not thereby redundant — its justification broadens.** The censuses already span **seven languages** between them: `en`, `ja`, `nl`, `tr`, `es`, `fr` in `loss_mitigation_forms`, and `en`, `es`, `nl` plus **`arc`** in `organizational_forms`; the `source_lang` enum admits thirteen. Japanese is the second-largest at 158 rows across 11 forms and is MS's alone; the eight Aramaic rows will need a specialist whoever joins. **No single reviewer will ever cover this census, and the arrival of an Ottomanist changes which rows are reviewable rather than whether the state exists.** `coding-checked` also covers the non-linguistic case — a reviewer who accepts the reasoning but has not obtained the archive reference — which applies to the 81 rows across both datasets still carrying `[verify]`.

**Note on `full`.** It is in the enum and currently unused. Most reading in this project is targeted — the cited pages and their context — which is `partial`. `full` should mean what it says, and a coder reaching for it should be able to say what the source's last section argued.

## 2026-08-06 — ai

A third dataset, `loss_mitigation_forms`; a package created and superseded the same day; and a terminological problem inherited from the source literature that the project should not adopt.

### Why a third package, and why not the obvious one

The request was for a dataset of cooperative pooling forms organised on WP1's four components. The obvious build — a WP1 facet of `organizational_forms` — was rejected before anything was written. WP1's components already exist there, and four of the coded forms (*ʿisqa*, *qirāḍ*, *commenda*, *asiento*) are cooperative sharing arrangements, so a facet split would have put one form's cells in two packages, created two sources of truth for `isqa LR3`, and turned the WP1-versus-WP2 differential prediction into a cross-package join.

The first build was therefore by **corpus**: `cooperative_pooling_forms`, scoped to arrangements mutualising a hazard across a membership. That package lasted a day. Reading Harris (2023) showed the boundary cuts across a three-way distinction he draws and states as an open question:

> "Risk can be mitigated in different ways: allocation, spreading or pooling. We are only beginning to understand under which legal and organizational frameworks and maritime risk environments were risk spread, as opposed to pooled or allocated."

Pooling-only excludes exactly the comparison he says is unresolved. The package was widened to `loss_mitigation_forms` — arrangements that mitigate loss **without an entity** — with `MC1 mitigation mechanism` (`allocation|spreading|pooling`) as the sort key. Every cell of the superseded package was verified present before removal, and `MC1='pooling'` recovers it as a query.

The governing rule across packages: **a characteristic lives in exactly one dataset.** Forms suffixed `_alloc` are cross-references to `organizational_forms` entries, coded here only on loss-allocation characteristics and never on the entity characteristics coded there. `organizational_forms` asks whether something is an entity; this package asks how loss is borne. The *ʿisqa*'s `LP1=0`/`TS1=0`/`CI2=0` is Udovitch's finding, not redundancy.

### What the coding produced

Seven forms coded from Harris 2023. Three results worth recording.

**`PR1` carries the timing argument as a coded contrast.** `general_average` and `averia_pool` code `0`, `sea_loan` `P`, `bottomry`/`respondentia`/`marine_insurance` `1`. Pooling needs no ex-ante price because all calculation is ex post, which is why it is ancient; a premium requires probabilities, which is why insurance waits until c.1300; the sea loan prices crudely — 25–33% for western Mediterranean round trips, 40–100% for the Levant — and sits between. Knight's threshold in three cells rather than a narrative.

**The two pooling forms separate immediately**, which is what a facet must do to earn its place. `averia_pool` is `PY0=collective-good` and `CN1=ex-ante-periodic`: a levy that bought an armed escort on which no shipper ever drew. `general_average` is `PY0=individual-indemnity` and `CN1=ex-post-assessment`: the owner of jettisoned cargo made good by the rest. Same mechanism, opposite on both cells.

**`RB3` is the only cell separating bottomry from respondentia** — `vessel` against `cargo` — and `RB1`/`RB2` had to be distinct characteristics because the sea loan splits them: peril risk to the lender, market risk to the borrower.

`PY0` itself was added the same day because the first coded form broke the scheme: `PY1 payout trigger` silently presupposed an individual draw, which the *avería* does not have. Well-formedness failure, mandatory split.

### A declared test, not yet runnable

`tontine` is entered and deliberately left `.NR` on `PR1`. If the hazard-to-pricing sequence holds, pooling forms should code `PR1=0`; the tontine is the candidate counter-example, since mutualised mortality risk with subscribers grouped into age classes is ex-ante actuarial pricing *inside* a pooling form. Were it to code `PR1=1`, pricing is orthogonal to mechanism rather than a stage of it, and the sequence above is an artefact of which forms happen to be coded.

Not coded, for two reasons that should be resolved in order. No source is to hand — Harris does not treat tontines, and coding it from general knowledge would breach this repo's own rule. And "tontine" probably spans pre- and post-actuarial forms, so a single row may not hold it and the type may need splitting by period.

### `hazard` and `risk` are not interchangeable, and the source literature treats them as if they were

**Harris writes throughout of "risk mitigation", "maritime risks" and "risk spreading" for institutions his own Knightian framework places under *uncertainty*.** General Average is his paradigm case of a device that functions where nothing can be priced — and he calls what it mitigates a risk. On his own argument it is not one.

The distinction the project should hold, and which the sources' actors largely observed even where the secondary literature does not:

- **hazard** — a peril conceived as an event that may befall, carrying no distribution over outcomes, not priceable, and met by avoidance, sharing or propitiation.
- **risk** — a peril conceived as a quantified distribution, priceable, and therefore transferable to a party with no stake in the venture.

The movement from the first conception to the second is the semantic shift the project describes, and `PR1` is where the matrix records it. Adopting the source literature's loose "risk" would make that shift invisible by naming its endpoint as though it had always obtained — the same error as reading a functional category back into a tradition that never articulated it, which is what the `articulation` column exists to prevent.

**Consequence, applied:** `PR1`'s name was written as "risk priced ex ante", importing precisely the looseness objected to here, while `HZ1`/`HZ2` already said "hazard". Renamed to **"peril priced ex ante"** — neutral as between the two conceptions, which is what a characteristic measuring the shift has to be. The code is unchanged. Added to the reserved terminology in `CLAUDE.md` alongside `genetic`, `convergence` and the units rule.

### Open

- `commenda_alloc`, `qirad_alloc`, `isqa_alloc`, `ortoq_alloc` are in the census and uncoded. Harris cautions against treating the *commenda* as a risk instrument at all — "dealing with risks is not its main purpose... it was also, even primarily, an employment or agency contract" — and coding them decides against that caution. Maintainer's call.
- Harris treats the business corporation as **pooling** (the EIC and VOC pooling uncertainties they could not price), which gives `joint_stock` a claim to a row here as well as in `organizational_forms`, on the same cross-reference logic.

That mismatch is arguably what the institution exists to correct, and a characteristic that can only say `mixed` about it may be under-specified.

## 2026-08-03 (ii) — ai

Two component sets declared, one per work package, and the reasoning behind splitting them.

**The prompt.** Mapping the application's five components (entity shielding, capital lock-in, transferable claims, legal personality, perpetual succession — Objective 2) onto the 32 characteristics left **19 unassigned**, including the entire liability-risk and contractual-form facets. `AP3` owner shielding mapped to nothing. Since those 19 contain every result the matrix has produced — the *ʿisqa*/*qirāḍ* contrast, the `LR2`/`LR6` split, the pooling facet — a falsification protocol stated over the five would have been blind to the instrument's own findings.

**The diagnosis.** The five are Hansmann–Kraakman–Squire's and answer *what makes a corporation*. The project's mechanism asks *whether an agent's trajectory can reach zero*. Those come apart at owner shielding: entity shielding governs creditor priority in the fund, owner shielding truncates the member's downside. The omission of owner shielding from the five is deliberate — limited liability was late and plural (Harris 2020), so including it would skew the corporate census toward late forms — and correct for the corporation question. It leaves the ergodicity mechanism without a coded expression.

**The resolution, which is theoretical rather than administrative.** The two sets are **one mechanism applied to two units**. Owner shielding removes the absorbing barrier from the individual's trajectory; entity shielding, lock-in and perpetual succession remove it from the entity's. Both are ergodicity-restoring; they differ in whose time-average is protected. This is why WP1 and WP2 cannot be separated, and it gives the work-package split a warrant.

**What was written.** `component` and `work_package` columns on the characteristic vocabulary. WP2 keeps the five across 13 characteristics. WP1 receives four — owner shielding (`AP3`,`LR1`), outcome coupling (`LR2`,`LR6`), loss sharing (`LR3`,`CF2`), risk pooling (`LR4`,`LR5`) — across 8. Eleven are `none` in both: description carrying no theoretical load. **The WP1 set is proposed here and is not yet in the application.**

**Two consequences worth recording.** First, the split predicts a *trade-off*: devices protecting the entity can trap the member, and `CI3` transferable claims is the hinge that resolves it — `waqf_khayri` has maximal continuity with `CI3=0`, `bazacle_mill` has continuity *with* `CI3=1` from 1182. Second, it converts the application's cross-regional falsification protocol from unfalsifiable ("traditions yield different functional combinations", which enough characteristics guarantee) into a differential prediction: the framework predicts *which unit* each tradition protects.

**Flagged, not acted on.** `LR3 profit-and-loss sharing` is a single ternary asking two questions — how gains are distributed and how losses are. The *qirāḍ* separates them completely and the *ʿisqa* by design. This is the same well-formedness failure as `LR2`, and the project's own working notes already call for gain-vector and loss-vector as paired fields. Two complications hold it up: a distribution key is a ratio rather than `1|P|0`, so this is a schema change; and recording both vectors would make `LR6` derivable, creating precisely the redundancy the dependence columns exist to catch. Maintainer's decision.

## 2026-08-03 — ai

The methodological literature standing behind the feature space, and the closing of two of the three open questions left on 2026-07-29. Operational rules distilled from this are in `CHARACTER-CODING.md`; decisions and version bumps in `CHANGELOG.md`. This entry records *why that literature bears*, which is the logbook's job.

### The problem the feature space has

Every argument below reduces to one fact. **Discriminating power is free.** With enough characteristics any two forms separate, so the observation that a characteristic "lets us tell X from Y" is not evidence about X and Y — it is a report on how many characteristics were used.

The formal statement is Watanabe's **ugly duckling theorem**: over the set of all possible predicates, any two objects share the same number of predicates, so similarity is constant across every pair unless the predicates are weighted, and no weighting is derivable from the objects themselves. Classification without a prior bias makes everything equally similar and equally dissimilar. For a Zwicky matrix this is not abstract: with *k* binary characteristics there are 2^*k* configurations, and where the number of coded forms is far below that — always — every form occupies its own cell by construction.

The consequence for this repo is **not** that the characteristic set should be cut. It is that the matrix does three different jobs under three different constraints, and a rule from one must not migrate to another:

- **Description.** No budget. More characteristics is strictly better. This is most of what the matrix does.
- **Similarity and distance claims** — clustering, "convergence pair", "A is more like B than C". Here the theorem binds, and *adding forms does not help*. Only a declared weighting helps. An unweighted distance computation over all 32 characteristics asserts that `FP3 identity wrapper` matters exactly as much as `AP3 owner shielding`.
- **Configurational or causal inference.** Here a case-to-condition budget binds. The matrix has no outcome variable and this use is not currently available.

An earlier version of this reasoning, in session, imported the QCA budget into the descriptive use and concluded the characteristic set was overgrown by two orders of magnitude. That was wrong twice — the arithmetic (the shortfall is ~20–25×, not 100×) and the category (a descriptive coding with no outcome variable is not a truth table). Recorded here because the error is the natural one and will recur.

### Character individuation — the systematics literature

The discipline that has thought hardest about when a character is well formed.

- **Sereno, P.C. 2007. Logical basis for morphological characters in phylogenetics.** *Cladistics* 23: 565–587. `10.1111/j.1096-0031.2007.00161.x`. The central paper: finds character independence routinely overlooked and no consensus on what a character is; decomposes any character statement into locator, variable, variable qualifier and state; shows the classic controversies — above all the state "absent" — are symptoms of *incomplete character statements*. Yields the operational test: **a characteristic containing two variables is ill-formed and must be split.**
- **Vogt, L. 2018. The logical basis for coding ontologically dependent characters.** *Cladistics.* `10.1111/cla.12209`. Directly on the `applicability_on` column. Should be read before the implementation is defended in print.
- **Vogt, L. 2017. Assessing similarity: on homology, characters and the need for a semantic approach to non-evolutionary comparative homology.** *Cladistics.* `10.1111/cla.12179`.
- **Hawkins, Hughes & Scotland 1997. Primary homology assessment, characters and character states.** *Cladistics* 13: 275–283. `10.1111/j.1096-0031.1997.tb00320.x`
- **Pleijel, F. 1995. On character coding for phylogeny reconstruction.** *Cladistics* 11: 309–315. `10.1016/0748-3007(95)90018-7`
- **Brazeau, M.D. 2011. Problematic character coding methods in morphology and their effects.** *Biol. J. Linn. Soc.* 104: 489–498. `10.1111/j.1095-8312.2011.01755.x`

### Similarity, and why systematics is more useful than philosophy here

### Whether two characteristics are one construct

- **Campbell, D.T. & Fiske, D.W. 1959. Convergent and discriminant validation by the multitrait-multimethod matrix.** *Psychological Bulletin* 56: 81–105. `10.1037/h0046016`.

The operative finding: discriminant validity **cannot be settled analytically**, only by observing cases where the constructs come apart. This repo has a worked instance. `AP3`, `LR1`, `CF2` and `LR6` were grouped as `agent-loss-exposure` on the argument that in bilateral capital-labour contracts they record one fact. Coding `asiento_averia` — multilateral, no capital-labour asymmetry — separated them on substantive values within a day. One form settled what no amount of reasoning could.

### Typology construction in the social sciences

- **Haspelmath, M. 2010. Comparative concepts and descriptive categories in crosslinguistic studies.** *Language* 86: 663–687. `10.1353/lan.2010.0021`. **The sharpest external objection available to this matrix.** Distinguishes comparative concepts, built by the analyst and belonging to no particular tradition, from descriptive categories, internal to one tradition and not exportable. Equating a descriptive category across traditions is illegitimate. Three characteristics fail this now and are flagged rather than repaired: `AP4` is written around a *founder* and a *corpus* and is waqf-shaped; `MG4=religious` for `isqa` was coded for consistency with `waqf_khayri`, which is exactly the prohibited move; `FP4`'s "political authority" does not map onto a corporate minority community. `bazacle_mill` sharpened the third — its authorization is neither `state-charter` nor `customary`, because personality came from received learned law while the mills were held by comital enfeoffment.
- **Collier, LaPorte & Seawright 2012. Putting typologies to work: concept formation, measurement, and analytic rigor.** *Political Research Quarterly* 65: 217–232. `10.1177/1065912912437162`
- **Sartori, G. 1970. Concept misformation in comparative politics.** *American Political Science Review* 64: 1033–1053. `10.2307/1958356`. The ladder of abstraction and conceptual stretching.

### Cell compression — the operational vocabulary we were missing

**Elman, C. 2005. Explanatory typologies in qualitative studies of international politics.** *International Organization* 59(2): 293–326. `10.1017/S0020818305050101`. Read 2026-08-03 from the supplied PDF.

Elman gives a **typology of the ways a property space can be legitimately shrunk**, with the cost of each. This is exactly the "declare an analytical core" problem, and his vocabulary maps almost cell-for-cell onto decisions already taken in this repo — which means those decisions can now be named and defended in the literature's own terms rather than improvised.

His five compressions, and where each lands here:

| Elman | Operation | In this dataset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rescaling** | reduce the level of measurement | The *inverse* of what `LR6` did — we raised resolution by splitting `LR2`, rather than lowering it |
| **Indexing** | weight variables and combine into a composite; equal totals treated as equivalent | **What an unweighted distance computation does implicitly and badly.** A declared analytical core is indexing done explicitly and on stated grounds |
| **Logical compression** | delete cells from impossible or highly improbable combinations | **`applicability_on` plus `.NA`.** Elman names and licenses what the repo already does |
| **Empirical compression** | delete empirically empty cells | **The move `.NR` exists to prevent.** Empirically empty is not impossible |
| **Pragmatic compression** | collapse contiguous cells whose division serves no purpose | What merging `AP2`/`CI2` would have been, had the *asiento* not separated them |

Two further points earn their place. Elman treats **expansion** as a first-class operation, including the *rediscovery of deleted cells* — which is the same warning as limited diversity, and the reason the distinction between `.NR` and `.NA` is load-bearing rather than fussy: conflate empirical with logical compression and the deleted cells cannot be recovered because the record no longer says why they went. And he warns against **reification**, "where the labels given to a typology's categories displace" the underlying combinations. That is the standing risk for `dependence_group`: `agent-loss-exposure` is a bookkeeping device for characteristics that partly co-vary, not an entity, and the *asiento* has already shown it is not even a stable one.

### The archaeology precedent — types discovered or imposed

**Spaulding, A.C. 1953. Statistical techniques for the discovery of artifact types.** *American Antiquity* 18(4): 305–313. JSTOR `277099`. Read 2026-08-03 from the supplied PDF.

Spaulding's position is stated in his opening paragraph and is the strong one: types are "groups of combinations of attributes favored by the makers of the artifacts, **not an arbitrary procedure of the classifier**." His method is to build contingency tables of attribute pairs and test their association with **chi square**, so that a type is a combination of attributes co-occurring beyond what random drawing from the population would produce. Classification must then be carried out "exhaustively and independently for each cultural context."

**Why this matters more than its date suggests: Spaulding offers an empirical escape from the ugly duckling problem.** Watanabe says similarity requires a weighting that cannot be derived from the objects. Spaulding's answer is that the weighting comes from the *data* — attribute combinations that recur beyond chance are real features of the makers' practice rather than artefacts of the analyst's attention. If that works, the matrix does not need a declared core; it needs enough cases for the associations to show.

**The catch is decisive for us.** Chi square on attribute pairs requires enough cases for the test to mean anything. With four forms coded across all 32 characteristics, it is unavailable — and will be for a long time.

So the Ford–Spaulding debate poses exactly the choice this repo faces, and poses it as a fork rather than a spectrum:

- **the Spaulding route** — let the data reveal which attribute combinations are non-random, and take those as the types. Needs cases, which we do not have.
- **the Ford route** — types are analytical constructs imposed for a stated purpose, and the purpose supplies the weighting. Needs a declared analytical core, which we have not written.

We currently have neither, which is the honest description of where the matrix stands for similarity claims. Ford's reply (*American Antiquity*, 1954) has **not** been read and the characterisation of his position above is secondhand; it should be checked before the fork is presented this way in print.

### Open questions from 2026-07-29, revisited

**(i) `.NA` treated as its own state in downstream views — still open.** Nothing has been built that renders the matrix, so nothing has yet violated it. `scripts/check_dependence.py` now enforces the related discipline at coding time: it distinguishes separation on substantive values from separation via `.NA`, on the ground that inapplicability is a fact about scope and not evidence that two characteristics vary independently.

**(ii) Whether `LR2` needs finer than three ordinal levels once the partnership types are coded — CLOSED, and the answer was yes.** Coding `qirad`, `commenda` and `isqa` returned `coupled` for all three, erasing the difference between an agent holding a call option bounded below at zero and a manager holding a debt that can carry him past it. The distinction survived only by accident, in `CF2`. Repaired not by adding levels to `LR2` but by splitting off **`LR6 coupling symmetry`** (`symmetric|upside-only|downside-only`) — `LR2` records whether the decision-maker is bound to outcomes, `LR6` records which way. This is Sereno's well-formedness test applied: `LR2` had been asking two questions.

**(iii) Whether provenance — transmission versus independent recurrence — should be coded columns or kept an analytic overlay — still open, and now sharper.** Recommend **overlay, not columns**, on the evidence of Le Bris, Goetzmann & Pouget's convergent-evolution paper. Their §VI.A shows the Roman *universitas*, as revived in the *corpus iuris civilis*, already carried entity shielding, delegated management and legal personality, and that a *pariage* was treated as a *universitas* and so acquired personality automatically. On their own stated criterion — convergence requires that the common ancestor *lacked* the trait — that is inheritance, and the case is **parallelism**, not convergence. They preserve the term by filing the Roman background under *environmental* features in the conclusion.

The lesson for the schema: **whether a shared trait is inherited or independently arrived at is a claim about the whole set of forms and their ancestry, not a property of any single form.** It therefore cannot be a column on a form × characteristic table without inviting exactly the reclassification error above. Keep it as an overlay with its own evidence, and keep the vocabulary's reserved distinction between `convergence` and `parallelism` doing the work.

## 2026-07-29 — ai

New dataset `organizational_forms`: a morphological (Zwicky) feature-coding of cooperative organizational forms. Design decisions, recorded from the design session; provisional, pending MS ratification.

**Long/tidy, not wide.** Stored one row per form × characteristic, so every cell carries its own `source_ref`, `confidence`, and `coder`. The wide form × characteristic matrix — and any radar, dendrogram, or lattice view — is a pivot of the long table, never hand-maintained. Per-cell provenance is impossible in a wide layout; that is the whole reason for the shape.

**Value vocabulary, and its mapping onto the repo's missing tokens.** Ternary characteristics are coded `1` / `P` / `0`; nominal and ordinal characteristics take one of their allowed categories (see `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv`). Three kinds of non-value are kept strictly distinct, reusing the project's missingness discipline: `0` is observed-absent, a datum; `.NA` is the characteristic being inapplicable to this form; `.NR` is not-yet-coded. This is the same move as the `clearing_records` missingness field — absence is disaggregated, never blank — applied to a categorical feature space.

**Value orthogonal to confidence.** `value` carries the substance, `confidence` (high/medium/low) the epistemic status, on independent axes. A contested-but-present cell is `value=1, confidence=low` — e.g. `joint_stock` × `AP3` (limited liability), late and plural per Harris 2020. `P` is reserved for genuine degree; scholarly dispute lives in `confidence` plus `notes`, never in `P`.

**Deliberate deviation from the enum-duplicates-vocabulary convention (CONTRIBUTING §4).** `type_id` and `char_id` validate as plain strings against the two vocabularies referenced in prose, rather than being `enum`-enforced in the schema, because the form census is about to grow substantially and a hand-maintained moving enum invites the vocabulary/schema drift §4 warns against. Referential integrity currently rests on the vocabularies plus a check script. Promote to `enum` or to foreign-key-linked resources once the census stabilises — flagged for MS.

**Open architectural questions.** (i) Confirm every downstream view (matrix render, clustering, lattice) treats `.NA` as its own state, never merged with `0`. (ii) Whether `LR2` outcome-coupling needs finer than three ordinal levels once the partnership types (commenda, mudaraba, qirad) are coded. (iii) Whether to add a provenance facet (transmission vs independent recurrence) as coded columns, or keep it an analytic overlay rather than a feature of the forms.

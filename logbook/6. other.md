## 2026-08-09 — ai (labour pooling, and a form-level independence problem)

**Nineteen forms and none pools labour.** Every coded arrangement moves money, goods or land. Reciprocal labour exchange — Korean 품앗이 (*pumasi*), Japanese 結 (*yui*) — is a pooling institution with the same functional profile and has no representation. 鈴木栄太郎 (1963) pairs them explicitly: 契 to 講 for financial cooperation, *pumasi* to *yui* for labour.

**Recommendation: no characteristic, no form, for now.** A "medium of pooling" characteristic would pass the comparative-concept test more cleanly than `RB3` or `AP4` do — it is defined in terms available to every tradition rather than borrowed from one. It fails test 4: no coded form separates on it. And the forms that would are not codeable — 結 from sources the team reads, *pumasi* not, for the reasons recorded against kye generally. Coding the Japanese half alone would seed a facet with one form and no comparator, which is how the `pooling` group acquired zero variance after the `waqf_khayri LR4` correction. **Code 結 and *pumasi* together or neither.**

**The boundary question is worth recording even uncoded.** Is bilateral labour exchange pooling at all? Between two parties *pumasi* is a swap — A owes B, the obligation outstanding and bilateral, no fund, nothing to be a residual claimant of. At three or more it begins to look like `MC1=pooling`. The form therefore probes where Harris's category starts, which the census cannot currently ask of anything. Note that `CN1`, `CN2` and `LS2` all presuppose a fund; `warichi_iwade` already codes `CN1=.NA` for a related reason, and labour exchange would be a second such case by a different route.

**One datum to carry if it is ever coded.** Suzuki records that in *pumasi* the value of labour is calculated according to the sex and age of the labourer — an explicit equivalence schedule, a unit of account without money. `CN2`'s three values (`equal`, `proportional-to-stake`, `proportional-to-capacity`) were written for money, and a tariff is not a proportion. Note also that Suzuki asserts all four institutions rest on the supposition that human beings are equal, two sentences after describing that tariff; the tariff is the datum and the equality claim is his framing.

---

**A separate and more serious problem, surfaced by the same material: forms drawn from one community are not independent observations.**

Echigo held *warichi* (Brown) and *tanomoshi-kō* (松永 1999) in the same province, and *yui* is general to rural Japan. Three pooling arrangements segregated by medium — land, labour, money — in one community. If a village runs a portfolio, the coded profile of each form is partly a residue of what the others already cover: a village that reallocates land periodically need not have its credit institution address the same risk.

This is the characteristic-level non-independence problem one level up. `AP3`, `LR1`, `CF2` and `LR6` were four cells recording one fact about the *ʿisqa*; three forms from one village may be one settlement of how that village handles shortfall. **Counting them as three cases inflates *n* exactly as counting four collinear characteristics inflated the evidence there.**

**Consequences, stated as a question for the maintainer rather than a change.** Description is unaffected. Similarity, clustering and configurational claims are not: any such claim over forms drawn from a single community assumes an independence the data may not have. The minimal fix is to **record co-occurrence** — which forms are attested in the same community — so independence can be assessed rather than assumed. That is arguably a `type` vocabulary column rather than a characteristic, since it describes the form's context and not its structure. Not implemented.

## 2026-08-08 (iv) — ai (particular average: a category the matrix's source does not use)

**Harris 2023 uses "general average" 58 times and "particular average" not once.** Every cell of `general_average` in `loss_mitigation_forms` is coded from him. The distinction is standard in the maritime-law literature — it appears across at least seven chapters of Fusaro, Addobbati, Piccinno and Dyble eds., *General Average and Risk Management in Medieval and Early Modern Maritime Business* (2023), all of which are in the maintainer's library — and it is doctrinally load-bearing rather than terminological.

**The doctrine.** Addobbati: general average is "'voluntary', corresponding to the damage consciously sought out with the intention of avoiding a more serious one"; particular average "is accidental, that is the damage from an irresistible or unforeseeable external cause." The first is indemnifiable for reasons of equity; the second "falls to the owner of the damaged goods on account of the maxim *casum sentit dominum*." Dreijer tabulates a third variety for the Low Countries — Small or Common Average, ordinary operational costs such as pilotage — plus Contractual Average and *avería de nación*.

**Why it may matter to the coding, stated as a question rather than a correction.** `general_average` is currently coded `PY1=realised-loss` and `RB1=all-stakeholders`. Both are true of *general* average. Neither is true of particular average, where the loss lies where it falls and no one contributes. If the coded form is "general average" in the strict doctrinal sense, the cells are right and the census is simply silent about a neighbouring form. If the coded form is meant as "the jettison-contribution institution" broadly, then it silently includes a class of loss the institution refuses to share, and `RB1=all-stakeholders` overstates its scope.

This is not a discovery about the sources; Harris knows the maritime law. It is a question about what the `general_average` row denotes, and only the maintainer can settle it.

**Three options. Option 2 taken; option 3 remains refused.**

1. Leave as is and record in `notes` that the row denotes general average strictly, particular average being outside it.
2. **DONE** — `particular_average` coded as a form, `LM-0196`–`LM-0213`. See below.
3. Treat the general/particular line as a characteristic. **Recommend against, and still refused.** It would be a maritime-shaped category applied to forms with no such doctrine, which is the `AP4` and `RB3` problem for a third time.

**What coding the null actually produced.** Thirteen of eighteen cells are `.NA` — no membership, no contribution, no fund, no payout, no duration. That is not a poor coding; it is the vocabulary reporting, correctly, that it was built to describe *arrangements* and that this is not one. `MC1` is `.NR` because allocation, spreading and pooling are three ways of moving a loss and this moves it nowhere; it is the absence of a mechanism rather than a fourth one. The discriminating pair is `RB1`: `capital-provider` here against `all-stakeholders` for `general_average`, the same voyage and peril with incidence turning wholly on deliberateness.

**A caution about what the null licenses.** `particular_average` should not be counted as a form when reporting *n* for diversity-budget purposes. It contributes almost no coded values and adds no configurational information; its use is as a reference point for `RB1` and `CN1`, and as evidence that the census contains a case the sharing criterion is meant to fail. Counting it toward case-to-condition ratios would inflate *n* with a row that is mostly `.NA`.

**A related gap with no cell.** Addobbati notes that the boundary cannot actually be verified — deliberateness is "an internal act" attested only by "the master who makes the damage declaration" — so it is "artificial and uncertain" and maintained anyway "to limit so-called moral hazard." `VF1 loss verification mode` records *how* a loss is established and cannot record how *strictly*. On Addobbati's reading the strictness is the operative parameter, and a tolerated leak "works as a tacit incentive to sail." Developed in the vault; not codeable as things stand.

## 2026-08-08 (iii) — ai (warichi)

**Two vocabulary gaps surfaced by `warichi_iwade`. Neither repaired.**

**(1) No characteristic represents an opt-out from the allocation mechanism.** *Hikikuji* let a shareholder withhold land from a redistribution. It ran from under 2 per cent of Iwade's arable in 1756–57 to 14 per cent in 1818, exceeded the 5 per cent Kaga domain permitted, and Brown records that "the rights to withhold were concentrated in a few hands, not universally shared" — which, he notes, "departs significantly from standard understandings of how warichi operated."

This is a third species of the pattern the census keeps turning up, and it is worth keeping the three apart rather than collapsing them. In the tontine the transfer toward the long-lived is *constitutive* — remove it and there is no instrument. In the bidding kō the transfer toward the liquid is *emergent* from the allocation rule — it arises from differential urgency meeting an auction, without anyone intending it. In *warichi* the advantage is an *exemption* — a right not to participate, held by some and not others. A device you cannot leave, a device that redistributes toward you while you stay, and a device you may exit are three different objects, and one characteristic should not be asked to hold all three.

**(2) `PY0` and `PY1` presuppose a queue.** Both were built on rotating associations where one member draws per round and then retires from the draw. *Warichi* allocates to **all** shareholders simultaneously at each redistribution; there is no turn, no position, and no waiting. `individual-draw` and `rotation` are right that entitlement is unconnected to any realised loss, and wrong that it is positional. Whether this needs a value, a split, or an `applicability_on` gate is not decided here.

**One methodological note for the record.** The analytical readings developed in the vault alongside this coding — that *warichi* spreads the *incidence* of an irreversible loss rather than reversing it, that it pairs functionally with general average, and that Brown's hazard variable measures exposure where persistence is wanted — are **not** in the data. They are hypotheses about the form, and the cells they would license (`MC1`, `HZ1`) are the cells held at `.NR`.

## 2026-08-08 (ii) — ai

**Two proposed repairs and one body of external evidence, from 並松 2022, 中川ほか 2013 and 有本ほか 2013. None implemented.**

**(1) `PY2 allocation rule` is under-specified for bidding.** It offers `auction` as a single value. 並松 sets out three distinct bid forms: (a) bid on the member's take (手取額), lowest bidder wins — 米谷's 引方多ク方 and 池田's 持寄融通社 are both this; (b) bid on the difference between the member's total 掛金+掛戻金 and the winner's receipt, which he notes gives the same result as (a); (c) bid on the **premium or interest** (割増金または利子), where the member willing to pay the highest premium or rate wins. (c) is a different object from (a) and (b): it prices the money directly rather than discounting the pot. Coding all three `auction` conflates a discount auction with an interest auction. A split would be a well-formedness repair in Sereno's sense, and it has a comparative payoff, but **the forms that separate on it are not yet coded** — so this is test 4 territory and should wait for one.

**(2) No characteristic records how long a member owes contributions.** `CN1` asks when contributions fall due. It cannot express the feature that defines `torinoke_mujin`: the winner is 次回以降の掛戻義務から免れ, released from all further contribution, while the association continues on the 未当籤講員. In every other coded association the winner keeps paying 空掛 to 満会. That is a binary structural difference with real consequences — it is why `PY3=1` here (the winner receives only 講金の一部, and the retained remainder funds the continuation), and it is one of the two grounds on which 大塩平八郎 had the form suppressed. Arguably `CN1` silently asks two questions, timing and duration, which would make the split mandatory rather than optional. Recorded, not taken.

**(3) External regulation is codeable and the schema has nowhere to put it.** From the 1930s prefectures imposed **floors on the winning bid** — 50 per cent of the payout in 鹿児島, 60 in 長野 and 三重, 70 in 山形・山梨・静岡・兵庫・愛媛・宮崎, 80 in 栃木. A floor on the bid is a ceiling on the implicit interest, and it targets exactly the transfer from the liquidity-constrained to the liquid that 並松 describes. Also: duration caps (島根 1914 ten years, 沖縄 1917 five, 大阪 1920 two, 北海道 1920 five), scale caps (沖縄: total 掛金 ≤1,000円 and ≤100口), 世話人 minimums (大阪 ≥3, later 山形 with a ban on holding the role concurrently), collateral requirements on 掛戻金 (徳島), and exemptions for kō among kin (福井・大分) or within a single workplace (島根). By the end of 1935 only seven prefectures had no 取締規則.

These are constraints imposed on a form from outside it, not properties of the form, and every existing characteristic describes the arrangement's own terms. Whether the matrix should carry an external-constraint facet at all is a question about what the dataset is for, and it is the maintainer's.

**Magnitudes now available, for the retention limb.** 農林省経済更生部『頼母子講ニ関スル調査』(1935): roughly 300,000 kō nationally, present in about 75 per cent of municipalities, averaging some 40 per municipality; **親有り講 = 66.3 per cent**; average total per share 427円, about 60 per cent of contemporary farm income, against other countries' ROSCAs at roughly one month's income; 77 per cent running five years or more. Mujin as a share of farm debt rose from about 8 per cent in 1912 to about 17 per cent in 1935. Litigation over 講金 ran 821 cases in 1907 against 2,991 in 1916, and 649 in 1920 against 9,566 in 1932 — counter-cyclical, surfacing in downturns.

**One comparative claim worth testing rather than repeating.** 有本ほか state that organising a kō for the relief of a *named* distressed person — 救済講, normally at zero interest — is, to their knowledge, not reported outside Japan. If it holds it is a genuine singleton; if it does not, it is the kind of claim that quietly becomes received. Either way it should be checked against the ROSCA literature before it enters an argument.

## 2026-08-07 — ai

**Proposed: split `PY1 payout trigger`. Not implemented; for maintainer decision.**

`PY1`'s allowed values — `realised-loss | life-event | rotation | need-assessed` — name the *kind* of triggering event and are silent on *whose*. Coding the tontine exposed it. There the trigger is another subscriber's death; `life-event` is the only available value, and `LM-0082` therefore reads identically to `LM-0097` on `widows_fund_scotland`, where the trigger is the member's own death. Two arrangements that differ on the single feature the ergodicity argument turns on — whether a transfer corresponds to the recipient's own adverse realisation — collide in one cell.

This is a Sereno well-formedness failure of the kind `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 1 makes *mandatory* to repair: one characteristic silently asking two questions, so its values are uninterpretable. It is the same failure that `PY0` was created to fix (`PY1` presupposing an individual draw) and that `LR6` was created to fix (`LR2` recording coupling without direction). The precedent in both cases was to split rather than to extend allowed values.

Proposed repair: retain `PY1` for the event kind and add a locator characteristic recording whether the triggering realisation is the recipient's own, another member's, or the fund's. Under it `tontine_en_1693` and `widows_fund_scotland` separate on a substantive value rather than via `.NA` — which `check_dependence.py` already treats as the stronger form of separation.

**Why it is not implemented here.** `CHARACTER-CODING.md` requires the component set to be declared *before* the coding it will be used on, and requires a PR to state which of tests 1–3 the addition passes. Adding the characteristic in the same commit that produces the result it explains would be retrospective selection — the precise thing the falsifiability commitment forbids. It goes to review as a proposal, on test 1, with `LM-0082` and `LM-0097` as the evidence.

**Also note.** `PY2 allocation rule` has no value expressing survivorship accrual: `rotation-lot`, `rotation-fixed`, `auction`, `indemnity` and `need-assessed` all miss it. `LM-0083` records `.NR` rather than forcing the nearest wrong value. Whether `PY2` needs a value or is subsumed by the `PY1` repair should be settled in the same review, not separately.

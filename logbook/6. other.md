**Two proposed repairs and one body of external evidence, from 並松 2022, 中川ほか 2013 and 有本ほか 2013. None implemented.**

**(1) `PY2 allocation rule` is under-specified for bidding.** It offers `auction` as a single value. 並松 sets out three distinct bid forms: (a) bid on the member's take (手取額), lowest bidder wins — 米谷's 引方多ク方 and 池田's 持寄融通社 are both this; (b) bid on the difference between the member's total 掛金+掛戻金 and the winner's receipt, which he notes gives the same result as (a); (c) bid on the **premium or interest** (割増金または利子), where the member willing to pay the highest premium or rate wins. (c) is a different object from (a) and (b): it prices the money directly rather than discounting the pot. Coding all three `auction` conflates a discount auction with an interest auction. A split would be a well-formedness repair in Sereno's sense, and it has a comparative payoff, but **the forms that separate on it are not yet coded** — so this is test 4 territory and should wait for one.

**(2) No characteristic records how long a member owes contributions.** `CN1` asks when contributions fall due. It cannot express the feature that defines `torinoke_mujin`: the winner is 次回以降の掛戻義務から免れ, released from all further contribution, while the association continues on the 未当籤講員. In every other coded association the winner keeps paying 空掛 to 満会. That is a binary structural difference with real consequences — it is why `PY3=1` here (the winner receives only 講金の一部, and the retained remainder funds the continuation), and it is one of the two grounds on which 大塩平八郎 had the form suppressed. Arguably `CN1` silently asks two questions, timing and duration, which would make the split mandatory rather than optional. Recorded, not taken.

**(3) External regulation is codeable and the schema has nowhere to put it.** From the 1930s prefectures imposed **floors on the winning bid** — 50 per cent of the payout in 鹿児島, 60 in 長野 and 三重, 70 in 山形・山梨・静岡・兵庫・愛媛・宮崎, 80 in 栃木. A floor on the bid is a ceiling on the implicit interest, and it targets exactly the transfer from the liquidity-constrained to the liquid that 並松 describes. Also: duration caps (島根 1914 ten years, 沖縄 1917 five, 大阪 1920 two, 北海道 1920 five), scale caps (沖縄: total 掛金 ≤1,000円 and ≤100口), 世話人 minimums (大阪 ≥3, later 山形 with a ban on holding the role concurrently), collateral requirements on 掛戻金 (徳島), and exemptions for kō among kin (福井・大分) or within a single workplace (島根). By the end of 1935 only seven prefectures had no 取締規則.

These are constraints imposed on a form from outside it, not properties of the form, and every existing characteristic describes the arrangement's own terms. Whether the matrix should carry an external-constraint facet at all is a question about what the dataset is for, and it is the maintainer's.

**Magnitudes now available, for the retention limb.** 農林省経済更生部『頼母子講ニ関スル調査』(1935): roughly 300,000 kō nationally, present in about 75 per cent of municipalities, averaging some 40 per municipality; **親有り講 = 66.3 per cent**; average total per share 427円, about 60 per cent of contemporary farm income, against other countries' ROSCAs at roughly one month's income; 77 per cent running five years or more. Mujin as a share of farm debt rose from about 8 per cent in 1912 to about 17 per cent in 1935. Litigation over 講金 ran 821 cases in 1907 against 2,991 in 1916, and 649 in 1920 against 9,566 in 1932 — counter-cyclical, surfacing in downturns.

**One comparative claim worth testing rather than repeating.** 有本ほか state that organising a kō for the relief of a *named* distressed person — 救済講, normally at zero interest — is, to their knowledge, not reported outside Japan. If it holds it is a genuine singleton; if it does not, it is the kind of claim that quietly becomes received. Either way it should be checked against the ROSCA literature before it enters an argument.

**`.NR` is doing double duty in `LM-0104`–`LM-0163`, and it should not be.**

`.NR` means *not recorded in the source*. Several cells in this batch carry it for a different reason: the vocabulary has no value that fits. `ko_usui_bango_1822 PY2` because allocation alternates by round and the characteristic admits one nominal value; `GV1` for every association, because the 講元/講親 convenes while a separate 銀預り (also 世話方) holds and lends the 掛銀 and drafts the 仕法, and neither collective, elder-fixed nor external-administered describes that split; `ko_mochiyori_yutsu_1876 RB3` because the pledge is a 田畑書入証 and the allowed values are `vessel|cargo|personal|none`.

The distinction survives only in `notes`. A reader working from values alone cannot separate "the source is silent" from "no cell exists", which is the same class of conflation the `.NR`/`.NA`/`0` split was created to prevent. **No token has been invented for it here** — that would be a coding-ontology decision, and the repo's rule is that those are surfaced rather than taken. For decision.

**`RB3` fails asymmetrically, which sharpens the comparative-concept diagnosis.** `ko_usui_bango_1822` codes `none` cleanly and articulated — 但シ、証文等入不申候 in the 仕法帳. The 持寄融通社's mortgage over fields and house plots has no cell at all. The vocabulary can express the *absence* of security and not this *kind* of presence, because its values are maritime. Absence is a comparative concept; presence here is a descriptive category imported from Harris. This is the `AP4` problem in a second facet.

## 2026-08-07 — ai

**Proposed: split `PY1 payout trigger`. Not implemented; for maintainer decision.**

`PY1`'s allowed values — `realised-loss | life-event | rotation | need-assessed` — name the *kind* of triggering event and are silent on *whose*. Coding the tontine exposed it. There the trigger is another subscriber's death; `life-event` is the only available value, and `LM-0082` therefore reads identically to `LM-0097` on `widows_fund_scotland`, where the trigger is the member's own death. Two arrangements that differ on the single feature the ergodicity argument turns on — whether a transfer corresponds to the recipient's own adverse realisation — collide in one cell.

This is a Sereno well-formedness failure of the kind `CHARACTER-CODING.md` test 1 makes *mandatory* to repair: one characteristic silently asking two questions, so its values are uninterpretable. It is the same failure that `PY0` was created to fix (`PY1` presupposing an individual draw) and that `LR6` was created to fix (`LR2` recording coupling without direction). The precedent in both cases was to split rather than to extend allowed values.

Proposed repair: retain `PY1` for the event kind and add a locator characteristic recording whether the triggering realisation is the recipient's own, another member's, or the fund's. Under it `tontine_en_1693` and `widows_fund_scotland` separate on a substantive value rather than via `.NA` — which `check_dependence.py` already treats as the stronger form of separation.

**Why it is not implemented here.** `CHARACTER-CODING.md` requires the component set to be declared *before* the coding it will be used on, and requires a PR to state which of tests 1–3 the addition passes. Adding the characteristic in the same commit that produces the result it explains would be retrospective selection — the precise thing the falsifiability commitment forbids. It goes to review as a proposal, on test 1, with `LM-0082` and `LM-0097` as the evidence.

**Also note.** `PY2 allocation rule` has no value expressing survivorship accrual: `rotation-lot`, `rotation-fixed`, `auction`, `indemnity` and `need-assessed` all miss it. `LM-0083` records `.NR` rather than forcing the nearest wrong value. Whether `PY2` needs a value or is subsumed by the `PY1` repair should be settled in the same review, not separately.

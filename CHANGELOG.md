## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **`cooccurs_with` and `cooccurrence_basis` ported to `vocabularies/organizational_form_type.csv`**, with **`same-institution`** added to the basis scale above `same-instrument` as its tightest level. `organizational_forms` 0.6.1 → **0.7.0**: vocabulary structure moved, and this repository reserves the minor for exactly that.

  The asymmetry this closes was real and it sat in the worst possible place. `loss_mitigation_type.csv` gained these columns so that forms drawn from one community could not be silently counted as independent cases; `organizational_forms` had no such facility, and it is where the `qirāḍ` / `commenda` / `ʿisqa` / `ortoq` cluster lives — the most tempting place in the repository to run a similarity claim and the worst place for undeclared non-independence. `ortoq_equity` and `ortoq_loan` are now declared `same-institution` in both directions.

  **No change to `check_vocabularies.py` was needed**, and that is worth recording: `check_cooccurrence` iterates every vocabulary generically rather than naming files, so the port inherited enforcement automatically. Verified by injecting all three defect classes into the new columns — a dangling code, a `cooccurs_with` with `.NR` basis, and a self-reference — and confirming each was caught, then reverting.

  `ortoq_alloc` in `loss_mitigation_type.csv` now carries `ortoq_equity@organizational_forms;ortoq_loan@organizational_forms` at `same-institution`, which converts a prose cross-reference into a checked one through the documented `code@dataset` form.

  **Not populated beyond the ortoq pair.** The obvious next candidate is `waqf_khayri` / `waqf_ahli`, two branches of one institution, and it should be declared when `waqf_ahli` is assessed rather than asserted now.

### Changed

- **Six `ortoq_loan` cells revised and one added, from Endicott-West 1989 (*Asia Major* 3rd ser. 2:2, 127–154), now read in full.** She fills the two cells that were parked as hers, and constrains two more.

  **`FP4` `.NR` → `1`, high, `articulated`.** 斡脱戶 *wo-t'o hu* is a named household-registration category in Yuan law, and the tradition names it in its own decrees — 通制條格 2 quotes imperial 聖旨 and princely 令旨 "concerning the sundry ortoy households". Registers stand behind it: a 1252 original and an 1263 supplementary listing 2,953 Muslim civilian households at Chung-tu. So does a bureaucracy with a datable life — Bōral Qarasun placed in charge 1253-01-09; 斡脱總管府 established 1268-01-13; 斡脱所 regional offices 1272-09-05; renamed 泉府司 1280-11-30; abolished 1284, reinstated 1285 under Dašman; given the Maritime Trade Bureaus 1286; regional 行泉府司 from 1288; **abolished 1311 "apparently forever"**, after which the ortoy were "comparatively free of restrictions and controls". The cell therefore describes c.1253–1311 and the row says so.

  **`MG4` `.NR` → `state-charter`, medium**, on 吏學指南 (preface 1301) glossing *wo-t'o* as the name whereby government funds for trade were disbursed to earn interest, and on the 泉府司's remit over "the expenditure and income of government funds for the emperor as well as the heir apparent, the empress, and the princes". Medium because of the source's own limit, below.

  **Both left `.NR` on `ortoq_equity`.** Endicott-West's apparatus is Yuan-jurisdictional and Enkhbold's equity evidence is Persian and largely pre-1260. Since the periodisation was removed from these rows in this same cycle, mapping the Yuan apparatus onto the equity form would re-import the sequence reading through the back door.

  **The limit that matters more than the cells: "no commercial contract as such has come down to us in the Chinese sources of the Yüan period."** The liability coding therefore rests entirely on Persian narrative — Juvaynī, Rashīd al-Dīn, Vaṣṣāf — and cannot be corroborated or refuted from the Yuan side. This reinforces the `low` and `medium` confidences set on the `ortoq_equity` liability cells rather than relieving them. Her closing is blunter still: the sources "do not allow us to answer such basic questions as whether or not there were rankings of any sort within ortoy merchant companies," and reconstruction is "hindered on all sides by omissions — both negligent and purposeful."

  **A second limit now recorded on `CF2`: her Yuan debt material runs the other way.** The 1318 decree on absconding debtors, the 1300 remission of interest for soldiers at Qara Qorum, and Wang Yün's debtors selling their possessions and their wives and children all concern the ortoq's **borrowers**, not the ortoq's liability to his principal. The two directions look alike on the page and must not be conflated.

  **`FP3` confidence high → medium, with the disagreement recorded as a coding.** Enkhbold has the restricting edicts "frequently ignored"; Endicott-West documents sustained curtailment — commercial tax from 1263 on "people who use government silver to engage in commerce"; household taxes 當差 explicitly not remitted per 1271 decrees quoting Möngke, with exemption available only by registering as a military household; no exemption from state service; weapons surrendered in port from 1293-09-26; and a 1309 memorial rescinding Muslim merchants' imperial letters, tiger tablets and post-horse rights, granted. Privileges then return after Qubilai and especially once the 泉府司 lapses. Kept at `1` because the form confers the arbitrage; **not** coded `P`, since `P` is a structural half-presence and not a value that oscillates over time.

  **`CF3` confidence medium, tension recorded.** Endicott-West describes the arrangement as one in which investors "pool their capital and entrust it to a manager", and the 泉府司 disbursed the funds of emperor, heir apparent, empress and princes through a single office — which reads multilateral on the investor side. Held at `bilateral` because she is glossing the *commenda* generally at that point and no Yuan instrument survives to settle it.

  **`ortoq_loan` `CF2`, `LR1`, `AP3` and `LR6` downgraded high → medium, closing the confidence asymmetry between the two regimes.** The asymmetry had tracked Enkhbold's own hedging — his loan-side statements are flat where the equity ones arrive under "it can be hypothesized" — but two limits apply equally to both. Endicott-West's "no commercial contract as such has come down to us in the Chinese sources of the Yüan period" leaves nothing on the Yuan side that could corroborate or refute the Persian narrative either regime rests on. And part of the loan-side claim is inference from the instrument rather than attestation: "A loan, by contrast, makes a borrower's liability unlimited" is a definitional step, not a datum. **No liability cell in either ortoq regime now stands above `medium`.** Thirteen of thirty-five ortoq cells remain `high`, and every one is structural or institutional — `CF1`, `LR2`, `LR3`, `LR4`, `MG1`, `FP1`, `FP3` on the equity side, and `FP4` on the Yuan registration apparatus.

  **`FP2` added as a deliberate `.NR`.** She describes a virtual state-merchant monopoly over the most valuable maritime goods — at Ch'üan-chou gold, silver, copper, iron and slaves were barred to private merchants — and the pre-Qubilai role of ortoy in tax farming 撲買, which she states did not continue into Qubilai's era. Neither satisfies `FP2` as written, which requires an exclusive right or franchise **held as property**; an exclusionary trade regime run by the state in its own interest is not a franchise the ortoq owns. `.NR` rather than `P` or `0`, because the question in `FP2`'s terms is not one the source asks.

- **`ortoq` split into `ortoq_equity` and `ortoq_loan`, coded on 34 rows** (`OF-0188`–`OF-0221`) in `organizational_forms`, from Enkhbold 2019 (*Central Asian Survey* 38:4, `10.1080/02634937.2019.1652799`). `organizational_forms` 0.6.0 → **0.6.1**: rows and census codes are additive and this repository has reserved the minor for schema and vocabulary changes (`source_lang` enum at 0.2.0, `LR6` at 0.3.0, the dependence columns at 0.4.0, `articulation` at 0.6.0). Codebook regenerated, 187 → 221 rows.

  **The split is forced, and the tontine row states the principle: a type that spans both values of a characteristic cannot be held in one row.** The ortoq spans both values of *four* at once. Under the early regime (Ögedei, Möngke) the principal bears losses capped at his investment and the agent bears none — Ögedei kept funding an ortoq through repeated failures (Juvaynī 1958, 209–210) and Rashīd al-Dīn 725 has investors unable to recover and trying to compensate their partners. Under the later Yuan and Il-khanid regime ortoqs were "completely liable for the loans they took, which extended beyond partnership capital to their private properties," with guarantors unconditionally bound and, on default, seizure followed by enslavement of the defaulter and his family (Rashīd al-Dīn 1998, 726). Enkhbold dates and explains the shift: the government spent 76,000 *ding* of silver in 1240 bailing out ortoqs, and "the huge losses suffered by Mongol elites motivated Mongol authorities to shift towards a risk-transfer model." Allsen 1989, 118 had already warned that the literature "may have confused two kinds of partnership capital: investment and loan"; the split is that warning made codeable.

  **The result.** `ortoq_equity` is **identical to `qirād` on all nine comparable cells**, and identical to `commenda` on all nine. `ortoq_loan` differs from `qirād` on five of nine — `AP3`, `CF2`, `LR1`, `LR6` and `LR3` — and differs from `ʿisqa` on only two of thirteen. A single institution therefore occupies both poles of the `agent-loss-exposure` group.

  **Correction to a first reading of this, made before the periodisation was checked: it is NOT a traversal.** Enkhbold has loan financing "adopted to finance business partnerships when Ögedei and Möngke ruled the Mongol Empire" — contemporaneous with the equity examples, which come from Ögedei's own reign — and only "eventually became prevalent … by the end of the thirteenth century." Equity and loan are **coexisting contract types whose relative prevalence shifted**, not a sequence, and the type rows are now unperiodised to stop the sequence reading returning. The 1240 bailout explains the shift in prevalence, not a change of form. This matters for what the case can be made to bear: two contract types available side by side within one institution is weaker evidence about lability than a documented movement would have been, and stronger evidence that the two configurations are alternatives a single commercial culture can hold at once.

  **Confidence downgraded on four cells after re-reading, and the reason is recorded rather than smoothed.** Enkhbold's own framing is that "Evidence of the liability borne by the Mongols or their partners is extremely fragmentary"; Lin 2013, 229 holds that it is unclear whether the ortoq had any entity or owner shielding at all, and Enkhbold contends against him; the generalisation is introduced with "Thus, it can be hypothesized that"; and the Fakhr al-Dīn leg rests on **Vaṣṣāf's silence** about the Ṭībī family's obligations. `ortoq_equity LR1` and `AP3` were coded `high` on that basis and are now `low`; `CF2` and `LR6` are now `medium`, since the Ögedei instance is stated flatly and corroborated by Rashīd al-Dīn 725 while the step from instance to form is not. Lin's dissent is recorded in all four notes. `.NR` remains defensible for `LR1` and `AP3` if the hypothesis is judged too thin.

  **This bears directly on the vault claim that the ortoq is the discriminating instrument, and it sharpens rather than supports the naive version of it.** If one institution can traverse the whole liability axis under fiscal pressure without leaving its own tradition, then a shared configuration is weak evidence of descent: the *ʿisqa* configuration is reachable from the *qirāḍ* configuration by local adaptation, with no contact required. That is direct empirical support for the vault's own diagnostic — *divergent arbitrary particulars*, not functional similarity, is what carries transmission. Note also that `ortoq_equity` cannot be distinguished from `qirād` on any coded cell, which is Galton's problem in its pure form: identity on a functional character set is equally consistent with descent and with independent arrival, and the ortoq had Uighur and Central Asian Muslim merchants in it from the start. The census cannot adjudicate the ortoq's origins and should not be asked to.

  **`TS2` is `.NR` for both regimes, deliberately.** Enkhbold: "The maximum time of validity of a Mongol–ortoq partnership is not entirely clear." Both readings are attested — the Polos free to leave once Berke's wares were sold, maritime merchants contracted per voyage, but also ortoqs "continuously employed by their Mongol investors, since the duration of these partnerships was at the will of the partners." `single-venture` must not be imported from `commenda`; that inference is what these two rows exist to refuse. Since `TS2` is one of the five cells separating `ʿisqa` from `qirād`, the `.NR` also means the ortoq's position on the duration axis is genuinely open rather than conveniently placed.

  **Endicott-West 1989 is not in hand and her cells are left `.NR` and named as hers** — `MG4 authorization source` and `FP4 legibility registration` for both regimes. Enkhbold reports through her that penalties for defaulting ortoqs were "confirmed in writing in 1295" and that reforms made ortoqs subject to official oversight and taxation, but oversight is not authorisation and a coding may not exceed a second-hand citation. `FP4` on `ortoq_loan` is the cell most likely to separate the two regimes once she is read.

  **One comparative-concept complaint, recorded not repaired.** `FP3 identity wrapper` is defined as conferring "legal-status or access arbitrage for its **principals**", and the *paiza* conferred it on the **agent** — relay mounts, provisions, and the tax evasion Enkhbold says edicts failed to stop. The characteristic is written around forms whose beneficiaries are their members; a bilateral capital-labour contract has no such symmetry. The same asymmetry shows in `AP3`, where "members' personal assets" has two referents in a capital-labour contract and they move in opposite directions between the two ortoq regimes.

  **`organizational_form_type.csv` has no `cooccurs_with` / `cooccurrence_basis` columns and therefore cannot record that these two are not independent observations.** `loss_mitigation_type.csv` gained those columns precisely so that forms drawn from one community could not be counted as separate cases. `ortoq_equity` and `ortoq_loan` are one institution in two contract forms — a tighter dependence than any pair those columns were built for — and the only place that can currently be said is prose in `key_source`, where no check can see it. The asymmetry matters because `organizational_forms` is where the `qirāḍ`/`commenda`/`ʿisqa`/`ortoq` cluster lives, i.e. exactly where an unweighted similarity claim would be tempting and exactly where non-independence would wreck it. Recommended: port both columns across, with `same-institution` added to the basis scale above `same-instrument`, and register them in `check_vocabularies.py`. Not done here — it is a vocabulary schema change and belongs in its own commit.

  `ortoq` is retained as an uncoded umbrella row and annotated, on the `tontine` precedent. `ortoq_alloc` in `loss_mitigation_type.csv` had its cross-reference updated to point at the two new codes and now records why it stays uncoded there: `LR4=0` in both regimes, so there is no pooling aspect for that census to hold.

  **The result is negative, and that is the entry.** Twenty forms become twenty-four and **no characteristic gains a state**: `CN1` 3→3, `PY2` 3→3, `MC1` 3→3, `PR1` 3→3, `MB1` 4→4.   Either the kō is one institution across two hundred and fifty years, or the characteristic set is blind to the dimension along which kō differ. The second is better supported, because the three authors differ from each other almost entirely on the **security and enforcement regime**, and `RB3` cannot express any of it.

  **`RB3` is the reason, and it is now the census's best-evidenced vocabulary gap.** `allowed_values = vessel|cargo|personal|none` were written around the bottomry/respondentia contrast, as the characteristic's own `definition` says. The pledges in these three sources are: arable land (三浦: 頼母子講維持策として絶えず田地を質として要求している, and specifically land yielding enough 得分 to sustain the 懸足, which makes the pledge a membership qualification rather than a security taken after the fact); town lots, storehouses and outbuildings (竹原屋平三郎's 表口16間、裏行き22間の屋敷と2棟の蔵, registered under the town officers' 裏印); rice in store quantified in bales (2000俵 for 小原屋武七, 500–700俵 for three others); third-party suretyship made mandatory for a stated reason (屹度請合人相立…若懸戻し銀不埒之時者、右請合人ゟ相究可申事, because 右質所売払候迄も、所ニゟ急ニ埒明不申方角も可有之候間 — foreclosure was slow in some jurisdictions, so the pledge alone would not do); and, by 嘉永5年, a member's own future draw (右は今後の自分鬮当金を引当としている). Five `.NR` now stand as the recorded motivation. Not repaired in this cycle: proposing the repair in the same batch as the coding that motivates it is retrospective selection.

  Note that `RB3` already separates kō on `none` — `ko_usui_bango_1822` is `high`/`articulated` for `none` on 米谷 2026, against associations that demanded a registered pledge. So the facet is not inert; the positive values are simply absent from its vocabulary.

  **`VF1` is now `.NA` on eight of eight kō.** Every one has `PY1=rotation`, so no loss is ever claimed and there is nothing to verify. The characteristic has zero variance across the entire Japanese material and is carrying no information about it. Recorded as a candidate for review, not acted on.

  **`MC1` is strained a fourth distinct way.** Coded `pooling`/`medium` for both Mitarai forms with the hedge on the record: 加藤's argument is that these performed a 商業資金調達機能 against the received image of 庶民金融 — 掛銀 of 1000–2000匁, 取銀 above 10貫匁, a third of the members rice merchants, returns of 1–6% he judges unattractive under 安永 and 天保 inflation, so that 一時に多額の資金を調達できるメリット was the point. What is mutualised is trading liquidity, not a peril. Not coded `.NR` only because the 安永2年 仕法書 carries a genuine liquidity-shock clause (御連中之内、不時ニ銀子御入用之儀有之候節者). Fourth form after `particular_average`, `warichi_iwade` and `ko_daikokuya_1848` where `MC1`'s presupposition fails, and the first where it fails because the arrangement is commercial funding rather than mitigation.

  **Allocation rule is not a period or regional marker, and `PY2` records that.** 御手洗町 ran lot-allocated and bid-allocated associations simultaneously: the 安永2年 無尽 draws by 鬮 with the premium fixed by rule at 500目 on 1貫目, while the 寛政11年「入札頼母子定法書並連名帳」 and the 天保11年 繁栄持寄講 allocate by 入札 every 4月1日. `ko_mitarai_gin_1805 PY2` is therefore `.NR` rather than filled in from its neighbours. `ko_yamakuni_1555 PY2` is `.NR` for a different reason: 三浦 writes 落札 throughout but his own note has 講親は初回に落札するのが慣例, so the 親 took round one by customary right and the term cannot bear an auction reading.

  `ko_hokinosawa_1820` supplies the **control case** for the bidding-kō transfer: order random, price fixed, the 刎金 set at one quarter by the 極証文 rather than bid. Two forms in the census now carry the auction mechanism and two carry its absence.

  **Two data the census has no way to hold**, recorded here so they are not lost. 法隆寺's 五十貫頼母子規式 has 縦雖有天下一同徳政、此懸支懸銭不可有改動之儀事 — the association's 懸銭 stands even against a realm-wide 徳政, and 三浦 shows that naming a kō for a religious purpose (極楽頼母子; 経蔵修理用途) was the device for obtaining that exemption. This is a hazard to the **fund**, where `HZ1`/`HZ2` ask about the members' peril. And 加藤's 銀頼母子帳 has 銘々私之取引有之候共、此頼母子ニて差次之儀者、一向相成不申事 — a no-set-off clause barring members' private dealings from the association's accounts, which is asset partition and belongs to WP2 vocabulary rather than WP1's.

### Changed

- **`loss_mitigation_forms` datapackage `version` 0.1.0 → 0.2.0, and the minor rather than the patch is deliberate.** Not because 76 rows were added — rows are additive and would have been 0.1.1. Because `RB3`'s `cargo` was **renamed** `goods`, which removes a state a downstream consumer may have been filtering on. At 0.x SemVer puts a breaking change in the minor; at 1.x this would have been 2.0.0. Repo-level release versioning (`CITATION.cff`, the dated `## [x.y.z]` headings below) is a separate space and is untouched — dataset packages advance independently, which is why `organizational_forms` sits at 0.6.0 and `clearing_records` at 0.2.0. Note this bump also carries the earlier unreleased `loss_mitigation_forms` work in this section, which had not moved the version.

  **CONTRIBUTING §5 has no rule for this case and should acquire one.** It says schema growth is additive — *add a field; do not repurpose an existing one* — and asserts that additive change never breaks a downstream consumer. Both statements are about *fields*. What changed here is a **value** in a controlled vocabulary, which the Table Schema cannot see at all and `frictionless` cannot police; only `check_vocabularies.py` can. Renaming a state is exactly as breaking as repurposing a field and the manual is currently silent on it. Recommended addition: value sets may be extended freely, but renaming or removing a state is a breaking change requiring a minor bump at 0.x and a major at 1.x, plus an explicit note of the old→new mapping. The mapping for this change is `cargo → goods`, one affected row (`respondentia`).

- **`RB3 security or pledge` value set widened, and it is the first characteristic that separates one kō from another.** `vessel|cargo|personal|none` → `vessel|goods|real-estate|surety|personal|mixed|none`. Decided by the maintainer as a separate act from the coding above, on the five `.NR` that coding recorded.

  **`cargo` renamed `goods`, and no new value added for commodities.** 小原屋武七's 2000俵 of rice pledged at 御手洗 is the same variable in the same state as respondentia's cargo: movables in trade securing an advance. The difference one feels — respondentia's goods are at peril, warehouse rice is not — is `RB4 discharge on total loss`, which exists to carry exactly that. A separate `commodities-for-sale` beside `cargo` would have split Mitarai from respondentia on a distinction imported by the analyst: spurious duplication, not repaired conflation. The bottomry/respondentia contrast the characteristic exists for is unchanged, now `vessel` against `goods`.

  **`real-estate` added** for land, town lots and storehouses. **`surety` split out of `personal`**, which was silently doing duty both for security on the borrower's own estate and for a third party standing behind him. 加藤 1998, 147 is what makes the split non-optional rather than tidy: the 請合人 is mandatory *because* 右質所売払候迄も、所ニゟ急ニ埒明不申方角も可有之候間 — foreclosure could not be effected quickly everywhere — so property and surety are two facts about enforceability, not two words for one. This is a test-1 conflation repair in Sereno's sense, the only justification `CHARACTER-CODING.md` accepts. **`mixed` added** for arrangements taking both.

  **Deliberately excluded: the pledge of a claim on the pool.** 松永 1999, 297: 右は今後の自分鬮当金を引当としている — a member pledging his future draw to 国田善徳寺 and 田中村八木家. `RB3` asks what secures an advance *from* the pool; there the pool is the collateral and outsiders are the creditors. Folding both into one characteristic reproduces the `LR2` defect precisely. Pledgeability and transfer of a membership share are transferable-claims vocabulary and belong to `organizational_forms`.

  **Recodings.** `respondentia` `cargo`→`goods`. `ko_mitarai_mujin_1773` `.NR`→`real-estate` (high, articulated): 鬮当リ之節者、当所連中之内慥成質物書入ニて、所御役人中之裏印を取 — a mortgage countersigned by the town officers. `ko_mitarai_gin_1805` `.NR`→`mixed` (high, articulated): mortgage deed plus mandatory 受合証文, over a 質所 spanning real property and rice in store, with verification zoned by place.

  **Two cells stay `.NR` although a fitting value now exists, and that is the discipline.** `ko_yamakuni_1555`: 三浦's 絶えず田地を質として要求している is a general finding about medieval kō, not an attestation for this association, whose surviving evidence is land *sales* by exiting members — default running its course, not a pledge clause. `ko_hokinosawa_1820`: the 極証文 records no security. Widening a vocabulary does not license filling a cell the source never filled.

  **Result.** Of 54 kō pairs with at least one comparable cell, those identical on every comparable cell fall **38 → 36**; pairs that separate rise **16 → 18**. These rows exist to make the contrast legible in the data rather than only in prose.

- `LR6 coupling symmetry` to `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv` (`symmetric|upside-only|downside-only`), coded for `isqa`, `qirad`, `commenda` and `waqf_khayri` (`OF-0088`–`OF-0091`). **`LR2` records whether the decision-maker is bound to outcomes; `LR6` records which way.** The gap surfaced during the *ʿisqa* coding: `LR2` returned `coupled` for all three partnership forms, flattening the difference between an agent holding a call option bounded below at zero (*qirād*, *commenda*) and one holding a debt that can carry him past it (*ʿisqa*). The distinction survived only by accident, in `CF2`. Since asymmetric exposure is what the project's ergodicity argument turns on, a feature space that cannot express it was under-specified rather than merely incomplete.

- **New dataset `loss_mitigation_forms` (v0.1.0)** — 78 rows, 7 forms coded, 25 characteristics, 20 in the census. Covers arrangements that mitigate loss **without an entity**, sorted by `MC1 mitigation mechanism` into Harris's (2023) three-way distinction: **allocation** (risk assigned to a named party by contract), **spreading** (transferred to third parties for a premium), **pooling** (mutualised among stakeholders, no outsider).

  The scope follows Harris's own closing sentence: "Risk can be mitigated in different ways: allocation, spreading or pooling. We are only beginning to understand under which legal and organizational frameworks and maritime risk environments were risk spread, as opposed to pooled or allocated." `MC1` exists to make that question askable of the data.

  Forms coded from Harris 2023: `sea_loan`, `bottomry`, `respondentia`, `marine_insurance`, `general_average`, plus `averia_pool` extended. **`RB3 security or pledge` is the single cell separating bottomry (`vessel`) from respondentia (`cargo`)** — otherwise identical. **`RB1`/`RB2` are separate characteristics because the sea loan splits them**: peril risk to the lender, market risk to the borrower, which is Harris's central observation about the form.

  **`PR1 risk priced ex ante` carries Harris's timing argument** and now reads off the matrix: `general_average`/`averia_pool` = `0`, `sea_loan` = `P`, `bottomry`/`respondentia`/`marine_insurance` = `1`. Pooling needs no ex-ante price because all calculation is ex post, which is why it is ancient; a premium requires probabilities, which is why insurance waits until c.1300; the sea loan prices crudely (25–33% western Mediterranean, 40–100% Levant) and sits between. Knight's threshold as three coded cells rather than a narrative.

  **The two pooling forms are separated by `PY0`**: `averia_pool` = `collective-good` (the levy bought an armed escort, nobody drew on it), `general_average` = `individual-indemnity` (the owner of jettisoned cargo is made good by the rest). And by `CN1`: `ex-ante-periodic` against `ex-post-assessment`. The facet earns its keep on two forms.

- `tontine` entered as the **declared falsification test** for the hazard-to-pricing sequence, and deliberately left uncoded on `PR1`. If pooling forms should code `PR1=0`, the tontine is the counter-example: mutualised mortality risk with subscribers grouped into age classes, i.e. ex-ante actuarial pricing *inside* a pooling form. Were it to code `PR1=1`, pricing is orthogonal to mechanism rather than a stage of it, and Harris's timing argument needs qualifying. Not coded because (i) no source is to hand and coding it from general knowledge would breach this repo's own rule, and (ii) "tontine" probably spans pre- and post-actuarial forms, so one row may not hold it. Recorded as a test to run, not a result.

### Considered and rejected

- **`cooperative_pooling_forms`, created and superseded the same day.** It scoped the corpus to hazard-mutualising arrangements only. Reading Harris 2023 showed that boundary cuts across his three mechanisms and excludes the comparison he says is unresolved, so the package was widened rather than kept. Every cell, characteristic and form was verified present in `loss_mitigation_forms` before removal; `MC1='pooling'` recovers the superseded corpus as a query. Recorded because the discarded alternative should stay auditable.
- **Splitting the WP1 facet of `organizational_forms` into the new package.** Rejected: it would put one form's cells in two places, create two sources of truth for `isqa LR3`, and turn the WP1-versus-WP2 differential prediction into a cross-package join. The rule adopted instead is that **a characteristic lives in exactly one dataset**; forms suffixed `_alloc` are cross-references coded here only on loss-allocation characteristics.
- **Coding `commenda_alloc`, `qirad_alloc`, `isqa_alloc`, `ortoq_alloc`.** Entered in the census but left uncoded, because Harris cautions against treating the *commenda* as a risk instrument at all: "dealing with risks is not its main purpose... it was also, even primarily, an employment or agency contract." Coding them would decide against that caution; flagged for the maintainer.

- **Superseded: `cooperative_pooling_forms` (v0.1.0)**, with `vocabularies/cooperative_pooling_type.csv` (10 forms) and `vocabularies/cooperative_pooling_characteristic.csv` (19 characteristics). Covers arrangements whose primary function is **mutualising a hazard across a membership** — rotating credit associations, general average, communal levies, confraternity funds, tontines — as distinct from the capital-holding vehicles in `organizational_forms`.

  **The split is by corpus, not by facet, and the distinction matters.** Coding the same forms' WP1 cells in a second package would have put one form's cells in two places, created two sources of truth for `isqa LR3`, and turned the WP1-versus-WP2 differential prediction into a cross-package join. Instead each package has its own characteristics — a *kō* would code `LR4=1` and nothing else under the old scheme, losing contribution schedule, allocation rule, default remedy and hazard correlation — while **both carry the same `component`/`work_package` vocabulary**, so WP1's four components are the shared analytical layer and the packages join at component level.

  Boundary rule: `organizational_forms` codes the vehicle, `cooperative_pooling_forms` codes the pool. Where an institution is both, the *units differ* and both are coded: `asiento_averia` (the contracting company, with its *partícipes*) stays in `organizational_forms`; `averia_pool` (the compulsory levy, with its shippers) is coded here. Their `LS3=0` and `AP3=1` describe two different populations shielded in two different ways, which is a finding rather than a duplication.

- `ko_mujin`, `huiguan`, `gongsi` and `huf` **moved** from `organizational_form_type.csv` to the new vocabulary. No data rows referenced them, so the move was free; after coding it would not have been. `huf` carries an explicit boundary doubt — a joint Hindu family is arguably a capital-vehicle — recorded in its `key_source` field rather than silently resolved.

- `PY0 pool output` (`collective-good|individual-indemnity|individual-draw`), **added because the first coded form broke the scheme.** `PY1 payout trigger` silently presupposed that an individual draws on the fund; the *avería* bought an armed escort, a non-excludable good on which no shipper ever drew. That is a well-formedness failure in Sereno's sense — a characteristic asking a question that presupposes an unasked one — so splitting was mandatory, not an embellishment. `PY1`–`PY3` are now gated on `PY0`. The episode is the argument for coding forms early: nineteen rows of one form falsified the payout facet within an hour of its being written.

- `scripts/check_dependence.py` generalised to any registered dataset, with a per-dataset vocabulary map and blocking rules for the new gates. It caught the `PY1`/`PY2` inconsistency above rather than my noticing it.

- `articulation` field on `organizational_forms` (v0.6.0), recording per cell whether the coded function was **articulated by the tradition's own advocates in their own idiom** (`articulated`) or is visible only under the analyst's description (`analyst-imposed`), with `.NR` where not yet assessed and `.NA` propagated with the value. This implements a methodological safeguard the application already promises — that functional coding be bounded by the sources' vocabulary — which until now lived only informally in `notes`. It is what makes *al-ghunm bi'l-ghurm* or *sekharo ke-poʿel batel* evidence rather than illustration.

  **Populating it caught an error in the first pass.** Twenty-two cells were initially marked `articulated`, six of which rested only on Udovitch — a modern secondary work. But `articulated` asserts that *the tradition said it*, not that a scholar reports it. `isqa AP3`, `isqa LR1`, `qirad AP3`, `qirad CF2`, `qirad LR3` and `commenda CF2` were downgraded to `.NR`. The sixteen retained each name a primary text (BT *Bava Metzia* 104b), a doctrinal term in the tradition's own vocabulary (*taʿbīd*, *shurūṭ al-wāqif*), or a secondary work quoting the clause verbatim (Hierro Anibarro on *"no han de estar obligados en mancomun"*). Current distribution: 16 `articulated`, 17 `.NA`, 122 `.NR`.

  **Recoverable:** the *qirāḍ* downgrades should return as `articulated` once re-coded with *al-ghunm bi'l-ghurm* as `source_ref` — the maxim is the tradition articulating loss-coupling in its own idiom, and would make `qirad` the worked demonstration that the safeguard operates.

- `check_articulation` in `scripts/check_dependence.py`, enforcing the weakest form of the rule: `articulated` cannot sit beside a `[verify]` citation, since a placeholder cannot evidence what a tradition said. Non-zero exit on violation, alongside the applicability check.

### Considered and rejected

- **Adding `articulation` to `clearing_records`.** Rejected: that dataset records transactions — dates, instruments, amounts, counterparties — not functional attributions, so the question "did the actors articulate this function?" does not arise for a coding that says a document is a *tegata*. Note also that `clearing_records` already implements the descriptive-category/comparative-concept distinction **by field design**, keeping `instrument_wareki` (the tradition's own term) beside `instrument_type` (the analyst's category). `organizational_forms` needed an explicit column precisely because its coding is functional attribution rather than term translation. The Methodology's phrasing — "the *functional* coding is constrained by the sources' own vocabulary" — is therefore correctly scoped as it stands and needs no widening.

### Added

- `component` and `work_package` columns on `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv`, mapping each characteristic to the application's declared component framework. **WP2 / Objective 2** takes the five components as they stand in the application — entity shielding, capital lock-in, transferable claims, legal personality, perpetual succession — across 13 characteristics. **WP1 / Objective 1** is given a parallel four-component set *proposed here and not yet in the application*: owner shielding (`AP3`,`LR1`), outcome coupling (`LR2`,`LR6`), loss sharing (`LR3`,`CF2`), risk pooling (`LR4`,`LR5`). Eleven characteristics are `none` in both — description carrying no theoretical load.

  **Why WP1 needed its own set.** Mapping the five alone left 19 of 32 characteristics unassigned, including the whole liability-risk and contractual-form facets — that is, every result the matrix has produced. `AP3` owner shielding mapped to nothing, because the five define *what makes a corporation* (Hansmann–Kraakman–Squire) while the project's mechanism is *whether an agent's trajectory can reach zero*. Those come apart exactly at owner shielding: entity shielding protects the fund, owner shielding protects the member's path. The omission of owner shielding from the five is deliberate and defensible for the corporation definition (limited liability was late and plural, per Harris 2020) but leaves the ergodicity mechanism with no coded expression. Two component sets, one per work package, resolves it without disturbing the application's five.

  **The unifying formulation:** the two sets are one mechanism applied to two units. Owner shielding removes the absorbing barrier from the individual's trajectory; entity shielding, capital lock-in and perpetual succession remove it from the entity's. This predicts a trade-off — devices that protect the entity can trap the member — and `CI3` transferable claims is the hinge that resolves it, letting the member exit by sale where lock-in forbids withdrawal. `waqf_khayri` has maximal entity continuity with `CI3=0`; `bazacle_mill` has entity continuity *with* `CI3=1` from the twelfth century.

- `bazacle_mill` coded across all 32 characteristics (`OF-0124`–`OF-0155`), from Le Bris, Goetzmann & Pouget (NBER WP 31821) and Sicard (1953/2015). Coded specifically to convert the Harris versus Le Bris/Goetzmann/Pouget dispute from duelling prose lists into matrix facts. Three cells carry the argument: **`CI3=1`** — pariers could alienate without the consent of the others, and the 1182 Castel enfeoffment already schedules transaction tax on halves, thirds and quarters, so Harris's "shares were not traded in impersonal stock exchanges" concedes the structural question and objects to the venue; **`CI2=P`** — the mills invert the joint-stock pattern, accumulating capital by call with forfeiture rather than by locked-in subscription, which is a real structural difference and the strongest thing in Harris's favour; **`FP1=mixed`** — deliberately unresolved, because Harris reads community provision and the others read profit-seeking, and this single cell carries much of the disagreement. `LP1=1` records personality as *inherited* from `universitas` without state approbation, which is where the parallelism-not-convergence argument bites.
- `asiento_averia` coded across all 32 characteristics (`OF-0092`–`OF-0123`), from the vault reading of Hierro Anibarro 2005 rather than from general knowledge. Preserves that article's own limits: `AP1` is **`.NR`, not `0`** — Hierro Anibarro never asks whether a *partícipe*'s private creditors could reach the *arca* (the word *acreedor* does not occur in the text), so coding an absence would assert more than the source supports. `CI3` and `CI4` are `P` pending Sayous (1902) on the extent of *transmisión de las participaciones*. `LR5=synchronising` is coded `low` because the vault note flags where this sits on the determinate–contingent typology as an open question.
- `scripts/check_dependence.py`: validates the dependence columns against the codings. Enforces applicability (non-zero exit), reports redundancy without enforcing.

- Three columns to `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv` recording **character dependence**, which the matrix previously left implicit:
  - `applicability_on` — the characteristic whose value gates whether this one applies at all (`LR5` on `LR4`, `LR6` on `LR2`, `CF2` on `CF1`, `MG3` on `MG1`).
  - `dependence_group` — characteristics encoding the *same underlying fact*: `agent-loss-exposure` (`AP3`,`LR1`,`CF2`,`LR6`), `legal-personhood`, `capital-immobility`, `duration`, `interest-alienability`, `pooling`.
  - `dependence_scope` — `always` where the members always collapse, `conditional` where they collapse only within a class of forms.

  These are two different relations and were deliberately not merged into one column: applicability is about whether a cell exists, redundancy is about whether it adds evidence. **The motivating problem:** the *ʿisqa*/*qirāḍ* contrast appears in four cells (`AP3=0`, `LR1=unlimited-several`, `CF2=1`, `LR6=symmetric`) but is one fact — the manager is a debtor on the loan half — recorded four times. Treating those as four independent characters quadruple-counts a single datum, the character-non-independence objection any referee with phylogenetic training will raise.

  **`agent-loss-exposure` was asserted with scope `conditional` and coding `asiento_averia` immediately tested it.** `LR1=limited` now occurs with `LR6=symmetric` (the *partícipe*, capped in both directions) *and* with `LR6=upside-only` (the *qirāḍ* agent, holding a call); `AP3=1` splits the same way. Both separations rest on substantive values, not on `.NA`. So limited liability does not determine the direction of exposure: the group collapses within bilateral capital-labour contracts, as claimed, and demonstrably not outside them. The *ʿisqa*/*qirāḍ* contrast still rests on one degree of freedom; the group as a whole does not.

### Notes for verification

- **Inconsistency surfaced by the new dependence columns, now resolved — `waqf_khayri` `LR4` recoded `P` → `0` (`OF-0019`, confidence `low` → `medium`, `source_ref` `[verify]` → Kuran 2011).** The form has no members and mutualizes nothing: the corpus absorbs risk and transfers income to an indefinite beneficiary class that contributes nothing and bears no share of another's loss. That is third-party absorption, not pooling. The decisive evidence was `LR5` being inapplicable — if the waqf pooled even partially there would be baskets whose correlation `LR5` could report, and there are none, so **the child's inapplicability is evidence about the parent rather than an anomaly beside it.** The discarded `P` was carrying Kuran's claim that waqf provision substituted for what became social insurance elsewhere: a claim about function, now recorded in `notes` rather than in a structural value.
- **Coding discipline this establishes:** `P` means the characteristic is genuinely half-present. Uncertainty belongs in `confidence`, functional analogy in `notes`. A `P` sitting beside a `low` confidence and a note arguing for `0` is almost always a `0`.
- **Consequence to watch:** with `waqf_khayri` recoded, the `pooling` group now has **zero variance** across all four coded forms (`LR4=0`, `LR5=.NA` throughout) and carries no information. This is a property of which forms are coded, not a defect in the characteristics — `asiento_averia`, general average and `ko_mujin` are genuine pooling vehicles and would break it immediately. Until one is coded, no claim should rest on the pooling facet.
- Within the `agent-loss-exposure` group the four characteristics are **perfectly collinear across all four forms coded** — no form yet breaks the pattern, consistent with their being one degree of freedom. Other groups show apparent collinearity across only two forms, which is not evidence: any two characteristics look collinear at n=2. `interest-alienability` has zero variance so far (`CI3=CI4=0` for both coded forms) and carries no information until a form with tradable interests is coded.
- All 51 new rows carry `coder=ai` and are provisional. The cells most in need of a human check are `MG4` for the *ʿisqa* (coded `religious` for consistency with `waqf_khayri`, but `private-contract` is defensible and the two carry different claims), `TS3` (coded `P` because the schema's single ternary cannot represent the loan half and deposit half separately — a limitation of the characteristic, not a partial state of the form), and `FP4` (the characteristic's "political authority" does not map cleanly onto a corporate minority community).

- `organizational_forms` dataset (v0.1.0): a morphological (Zwicky) feature-coding of cooperative and risk-pooling organizational forms against a granular characteristic set, stored long/tidy (one row per form × characteristic). Provisional and partly assistant-coded (`coder=ai`, `source_ref=[verify]` where citations are unpinned), pending human verification. Seed content: `waqf_khayri` coded across all characteristics plus illustrative cells for `joint_stock`, `natie`, and `nacion_cofradia`.
- `vocabularies/organizational_form_type.csv` and `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv`: controlled vocabularies for the two coded fields (`type_id`, `char_id`). Referenced in prose from the schema; `type_id`/`char_id` are deliberately not yet `enum`-enforced while the form census grows.

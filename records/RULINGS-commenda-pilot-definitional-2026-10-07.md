# Rulings on the commenda pilot's four definitional questions (2026-10-07)

**Status: ruled by MS in chat on 2026-10-07, in the session that opened the Crown of Aragon comanda batch. Recorded here by the assistant, verbatim to the options MS chose. The 24 cells in `ADJUDICATION-commenda-pilot-2026-09-28.md` are NOT adjudicated by this file: each still needs its own ruling, which these four now constrain.** The questions are those pre-listed in the worksheet, under "Pre-listed definitional questions".

## 1. Formulary absence — two-limb rule

A clause that the same notary writes into other instruments but not into this one is an observed absence (`0` / `none`) only when both limbs hold:

- (a) the same notary, or the same formulary, demonstrably uses the clause in other instruments in the same register, so its absence is a drafting choice and not a recording gap; and
- (b) the characteristic asks about the instrument's own terms — what it secures, what it binds, what it allocates — and not about default law outside it.

Questions about default law outside the instrument (juridical personhood, outside creditors' reach, pooling across members) stay `.NR` on the acts' silence. A cell coded under this rule carries at most `medium` confidence and `articulation=analyst-imposed`, and its note says that the rule was applied.

Bears on worksheet entries 19 and 21 (`RB3`), and on the `LP1` and `commenda LR4` disputes (entries 4, 8, 11), where it supports `.NR`.

## 2. Minority clauses — no minority `P`

A clause present in a minority of acts does not make a cell `P`. Code the modal state, record the counts in `notes`, and lower `confidence`. Where the minority is a coherent drafting variant, propose a split rather than a `P`. This applies the standing rule in `CLAUDE.md` that `P` is structural half-presence within one arrangement, never heterogeneity across instances.

Bears on entries 12 (`societas_maris LP2`) and 17 (`societas_maris LR4`), and on the four stage-4 `P` cells carried as scoring note 2 in logbook 4, 2026-09-28.

## 3. `commenda CI1` — live `common` stands; vocabulary question logged

Live `common` stands for `commenda`. Arm S's `none` rests on Udovitch 1962, 198, a statement about the Islamic-law contract, carried into the Latin case; that is a comparative-concept hygiene problem and is not adopted.

## 4. `VF1` / `VF2` — the acts' silence is `.NR`

A contract is written before any loss, so the acts' silence on how a loss is established is `.NR`, never `none` (which would assert that no verification occurs and force `VF2` to `.NA`). `VF1` and `VF2` are coded from the normative or judicial layer — statutes, customs, court records — and `source_class` records which. Where only acts are held, both are `.NR`.

Bears on entries 16 and 20 (`commenda_alloc VF1`, `VF2`). Whether live `documentary` (from the Dubrovnik statute, Held 2025) may stand for `commenda_alloc` is a separate question, because it imports a neighbouring jurisdiction's statute; that remains open in entry 16.

## 1. Exposure

**Kō in Japan, 1900s–1940s.** General textbook knowledge only, from training, nothing tied to a specific rulebook. What I carry: 頼母子講 / 無盡講 are rotating funds in which members pay 掛金 at each 會 and one member per round takes the pot (by 入札 or 抽籤/鬮); a member who has taken (取濟・既取) keeps paying, usually at a higher rate (掛戻し), while those who have not yet taken (未取) carry the fund's exposure to the drawn members; drawn members are commonly required to give 保證人 and/or 擔保 (often 不動産 書入); the 親 or 講元 convenes and often takes the first pot; 世話人 / 世話方 run collection. 模合 is the Okinawan name. I have a general impression, not attached to any source I can name, that deficits caused by a drawn member's default were in practice often put on the 親 or 世話人 ("親の責任"), and less often shared among 未取 members as a shortfall in the pot. I have a vague and unverified recollection that 大審院 case law debated whether a 頼母子講 is a 民法上の組合 (which would carry joint liability) or an 無名契約, and that it did not settle as 組合; I would not rely on this and it does not enter any cell.

**These two items.** Nothing specific about the contents. I know (training, general) that the Finance Ministry surveyed 無盡 in the run-up to the 無盡業法 of 1915, and I take the 1915 『無尽ニ関スル調査』 to be that survey or its kin; I know that 司法省調査部 issued a 『世態調査資料』 series in wartime on customs and social conditions. I have no knowledge of any rulebook printed in either, nor of which places they come from. From `FRAMES.md` alone I know the structure: an appendix of eleven rulebooks (M), an Aomori appendix of 講規約及證書 including 十錢講 and 信用會 rules that are out of scope, and a Tokushima 附錄 of three 講則 with 講通帳, 大手形, 小手形.

**Regulation.** General only: the 無盡業法 (大正4年) licensed and supervised commercial 營業無盡 and left 親睦 / 共済 kō among members largely outside it; prefectural 取締規則 existed before and alongside it (FRAMES names a Tokushima 講會取締規則, which I may not read). The later path 無盡會社 → 相互銀行 (1951) I know as a commonplace. Nothing on the contents of any 取締規則.

**From the bundle itself.** The bundle's name says this is a *re-test*, so an earlier test of a residual-default characteristic exists; I have not seen it, its codings or its result. The vocabulary carries two CLARIFIED rulings of 2026-10-22 (CN3, LS3) arising from "residual-default" rulings, and the existing `ko_*` rows show the census's house practice: heavy `.NR`, no positive `GV1` except where a document puts decision in an assembled body or an outside lord, `LS1` coded only once (`ko_mochiyori_yutsu_1876 = pro-rata`, on a layered sureties-then-remaining-sealers sequence). That one row is the nearest existing analogue to what `RD-test` asks, and I note that it would be `RB3`-side (sureties by signing) under `RD-test` rule 1.

## 2. Expectations by characteristic, over the nineteen

- `RD-test`: `.NR` in about 11–13; `officers` in about 2–3; `all-members` in about 2; `deferred` in about 1–2; `fund` in about 1; `standing-subgroup` in 0; `.NA` in 0–2. Why: a rulebook drafted by members typically specifies the drawn member's security and his guarantors' duty and stops there, treating the guarantor as the end of the chain; a clause naming a bearer beyond that is the exception, and where it exists I expect it to fall on the 講元/親 or 世話人 (convener as guarantor of the fund) more often than on the members, with a "協議ノ上" type clause in a minority. A pre-set liable group by membership (`standing-subgroup`) I expect not to see, because kō subgroups, if any, are usually for collection, not liability.
- `RB3`: `surety` in about 7–8; `mixed` in about 5–6; `real-estate` in about 2; `.NR` in about 3; `none` 0; `.NA` 0–2. Why: a 保證人 requirement for drawn members is the commonest clause I expect in rulebooks of this period, often combined with a 書入 / 擔保 option, and the Tokushima 大手形 suggests a formal bond with guarantors.
- `LS1`: `.NR` in about 15–16; `residual-on-fund` in about 1; `pro-rata` in about 1–2 (shortfall borne per 口); `equal` 0–1; `capped` 0; `.NA` 0–2. Why: few rulebooks will say how an unmet loss is divided at all, and where they do it will usually be the convener bearing it (which is a *who*, not a *how*, and leaves `LS1` `.NR`).
- `LS3`: `.NR` in about 12; `1` in about 5; `0` in about 0–1; `P` 0; `.NA` 0–2. Why: under the 2026-10-22 ruling a guarantor-plus-pledge clause alone is `.NR`; `1` needs either members answering for one another (guarantors required to be members, or joint liability of the membership) or an express 身代限 / whole-estate liability, and I expect a minority of rulebooks, perhaps more of the 1915 set, to require that 保證人 be 講員 or to make liability 連帶.
- `CN3`: `1` in about 15–16; `P` in about 1–2 (forfeiture of the draw or 除名 only); `.NR` in about 1–2; `0` 0; `.NA` 0. Why: almost every rulebook will either require security from a drawn member (which is `1` by the ruling) or impose 延滞利息, 違約金 or 除名 on late payment.
- `GV1`: `.NR` in about 10–11; `collective` in about 5–6 (a 總會 / 講員協議 deciding); `external-administered` in about 1–2 (a commercial 營業者 running the fund); `elder-fixed` in about 1. Why: the house practice already shows the value set fits kō badly (convener-plus-steward, insider-organiser), and I expect most rulebooks to name a 講元 and 世話人 without putting decision clearly in a meeting.

## 3. Expectations by source

- **1915 set (M01–M11).** Selected by the Finance Ministry's banking section while it was preparing to regulate 無盡; I expect it to over-represent formally drafted rules with numbered articles and to include one or more commercial 營業無盡 schemes run by a promoter or company, where the operator guarantees the members (`RD-test = officers`, `GV1 = external-administered`). I expect `RB3 = mixed` and `CN3 = 1` to be denser here than elsewhere, and more `.NR` on `RD-test` than the ministry's purpose might suggest, because a rulebook need not say what happens after the guarantor fails.
- **Aomori set (A03–A07).** Selected by the Justice Ministry for a survey of customs, with bonds and receipts printed alongside; I expect shorter, rural, customary rules, more silence (`.NR`) across the board, possibly a savings-type 講 among the five (hence a chance of `.NA` on `RD-test` and `RB3`), and the bonds (其ノ八–十) to carry the security clauses the rules omit, if they name the same association.
- **Tokushima set (T01–T03).** Three 講則 with a passbook and two classes of 手形: I expect the most formalised security of the three sets (a 大手形 for the drawn member's whole remaining obligation signed by guarantors, 小手形 per instalment), therefore `RB3 = surety` or `mixed`, `CN3 = 1`, and the best chance of a `連帶` clause that makes `LS3 = 1`. That they sit next to a prefectural 取締規則 suggests the three were drafted under, or with an eye to, that regulation, and may resemble one another for that reason rather than independently.

## 4. Row-level expectations

- **How many state who bears a drawn member's default beyond his own security?** About 6 of the 19 (range 4–8).
- **Most frequent `RD-test` value:** `officers` (convener or 世話人 answerable for the deficit). **Not at all:** `standing-subgroup`.

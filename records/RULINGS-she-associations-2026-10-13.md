## 1. Scope and unit — one `loss_mitigation_forms` row, `she_death_aid`; the 義聚 loan stock is evidence, not a row

One row, code `she_death_aid`, "*She* association death mutual aid (社邑喪葬互助), Dunhuang"; tradition Chinese; period second half of the 9th century to the 10th. It codes the members' obligations to one another at a death, from the death clauses of the practical bylaws (社條), the death circulars (身亡轉帖) and the death contribution registers (身故納贈曆) in Ning and Hao 1997. Practical documents (實用文書) are evidence; models (文樣), drafts (稿) and copies (抄), students' exercises included, are recorded as variants and never counted as cases. The unit is the practice across Dunhuang's associations, not each association: where associations differ, the cell takes the modal state and the note gives the counts. Excluded: the Turfan bylaws of 658 (comparison only); the irrigation associations (渠社, 渠人), recorded as a boundary note; the officially levied contribution registers (社司納贈曆, e.g. S. 3978), recorded in a note on `MB3`. **(a)** The 義聚 loan stock (the loan registers, 社司便物曆) is not a second row: lending a stock to members is credit, not the pooling of members' losses. It is read for `CN1`, `LS1` and `LS2` (whether a standing reserve exists beside the levy at a death); if the registers show it serving funerals it returns to MS, otherwise it is parked in logbook 2. **(b)** An `organizational_forms` row is deferred (decided with the brief). **Strongest objection answered:** one row hides the variation between associations, the women's associations being the best described; the census codes forms, the variation is counted in the notes, and a split is warranted only if the women's bylaws separate on cells, to be seen at item 4 (precedent: the commenda family's split, ruled on evidence after coding).

## 11. Co-occurrence and the type row (item 5)

**(a) The brief's two declarations cannot be carried by the co-occurrence column, in either direction.** `cooccurs_with` / `cooccurrence_basis` record forms attested together (`same-instrument` to `same-polity`; logbook 6, 2026-08-09 (ii)); resemblance and functional comparison are not co-occurrence. `huiguan` (16th century onward, uncoded) shares no community, persons, instrument or polity with 9th–10th-century Dunhuang; the guild rows are European. So `she_death_aid` takes `cooccurs_with` `.NR`, `cooccurrence_basis` `.NR`; the declarations go verbatim into its `key_source` and logbook 2 (`huiguan`: resemblance only, never descent, any likeness parallelism until descent is argued; the guild death-benefit rows: functional comparison only). Nothing is written on `huiguan` or the guild rows (the column does not apply; type rows are append-only). `.NR` here means "assessed, and no", which the column cannot say: third instance of logbook 6, 2026-09-04 (ii), strain 2, after `warichi_iwade` and `bruderschaft_salzburg`. What does co-occur is not a row (the 義聚, ruling 4a; the irrigation 渠社, boundary note; the official levy S. 3978, ruling 1); forward note: a future `organizational_forms` *she* row would be `same-institution`.

**(b) Non-independence inside the row, declared.** The cells count documents, not associations. Not independent: circulars 43 and 44 (one 兄弟社, 988); circular 27 and register 219 (one association, 944 and 949); copies 40 and 41 (one association, editors); registers 229 and 253 (possibly one, editors). Ning and Hao is the only witness to the texts; Teiser 2020 and Hao 2021 were not read for coding, so no cell rests on a second witness. To the `key_source` as the row's main limitation.

**(c) `period` widened** from ruling 1's "second half of the 9th century to the 10th" to **"Tibetan period (before 848) to c. 1000"**: circular 21, register 214 (P. 5003) and re-demand 151 date from the Tibetan period; the latest dated documents are c. 997 (registers 229, 253).

**(d) `boundary_basis` = `contemporary-terminology`, `boundary_confidence` = medium.** The boundary follows the scribes' own document types: death circulars (身亡轉帖) apart from banquet and blessing circulars, contribution registers (納贈曆) apart from loan registers (便物曆); Ning and Hao's sections follow the manuscripts' titles. Medium because the contemporaries' 社 covers the whole association and the split from the 義聚 is partly the analyst's (ruling 1a). Alternative `analyst-split`, medium.

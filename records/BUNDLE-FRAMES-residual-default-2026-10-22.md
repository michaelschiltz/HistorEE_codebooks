# Reading the source

農林省経済更生部『農村金融資料 第4号 賴母子講規約例』 (昭和11–13), National Diet Library Digital Collections, pid 1074152. **Public domain**, open without login. It prints forty-three kō rulebooks with an editorial 解説 before each, footnoted glosses, and sometimes the forms used with them. You read **seventeen** of them, and only these.

## How to read a page

The pages are images, read in Chrome. Use **only** URLs of these two forms, on **dl.ndl.go.jp** and nowhere else:

- the viewer: `https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1074152/1/<frame>`;
- the IIIF image service, which gives a sharper view of a region: `https://dl.ndl.go.jp/api/iiif/1074152/R<frame, 7 digits>/pct:<x>,<y>,<w>,<h>/,1600/0/default.jpg`. For example `R0000014/pct:46,5,36,92/,1600/0/default.jpg` is the right-hand page of frame 14 at a legible size, and `pct:12,5,37,92` is the left-hand page. Zoom further into a strip of columns for the footnotes.

Each frame is a two-page spread. The book is set vertically and read right to left, so on frame *f* the **right** page is printed page 2*f* − 14 and the **left** page is 2*f* − 13. Cite **printed** page numbers, and the rules' own article numbers (第何條, or the order of the 一、 items where they are unnumbered).

You may also read **frames 2–6**: the title, the table of contents and the 凡例. Read the 凡例 before any rulebook, because it says how the rules were chosen, transcribed and anonymised.

## What you must not read

- **Any other kō.** A kō often begins or ends mid-page, so many of your frames also carry the end or start of a kō that is not on the list. Read only the columns of the kō you are coding. When you reach a heading 第何講 that is not on the list, stop reading that page. If you read part of another kō by accident, say which, and what you saw, in your notes. Do not use it.
- **Any frame not listed**, and any NDL page other than these two URL forms: no search, no bibliographic record, no other item.
- **Any other website**, and any source on kō other than these pages.

## What the editor tells you, and what he does not

The 解説 is the ministry's summary and the headings carry the ministry's classes. **Code from the clauses and the forms, not from the 解説 or the class.** Use the 解説 for facts the rules do not state, such as where the kō was, when it began and whether it was still running, and say so in the note when you do. **Record each kō's village or town** as printed in its heading or 解説.

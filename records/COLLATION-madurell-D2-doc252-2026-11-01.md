## What it says on profit, loss and risk

- **Parties.** «lo senyer n'Anthoni Postomi comana per pura comanda a'n en Lorenç Belloch, mercer»: Postomi is the investor and Belloch, a mercer, the agent, selling the goods retail (*per vendra a manut*) in Postomi's shop.
- **Capital.** Mercery, silk ribbon and other goods «en vàlua de .L. lliures barsaloneses», valued by both parties at a just price; Postomi tops the stock up to 50 lliures as it sells.
- **Profit.** «per los trabals e temps que dit Lorenç Belloch met per vendra dita roba, haja la maytat del guany»: half the profit to the agent, for his labour and time.
- **Charges.** Shop rent of 6 lliures a year, and one sou a day for the agent's keep, borne «per maytat», six diners each, both paid out of the profit.
- **Account and term.** A nightly account on oath with a book of sales; three years, the investor free to reclaim the comanda and his goods if the agent does not give a good account, and to call for an account at any time.
- **The only clause on a shortfall.** «si dites sinquanta lliures se trobaven ésser més o menys, hajen star dites parts en los libres que ells abdosos menen, ço és quascún d'els son libre»: if the fifty lliures are found to be more or less, the parties are to abide by the books each of them keeps. It settles a discrepancy in the stock by the parties' books; it does not allocate a loss.
- **Security.** Each party binds all his goods and swears.

**No clause allocates loss or risk.** The census's `loss_rule = not-stated` for doc 252 stands.

## The study's citation

García Sanz cites doc 252, with docs 201 and 247, for risk divided in the proportion of profit, «muy señaladamente cuando era a medias» (p. 130, n. 229; collation, section 3). For doc 252 the citation is **not supported by the text**: the act divides profit and the agent's keep by halves and says nothing of loss. His rule may be his inference from the half share. Docs 201 and 247 state shared loss in terms (`records/instances-2026-10-27.csv`), so the census's two `comanda_ad_societatem` acts are unaffected, and **no third is added**.

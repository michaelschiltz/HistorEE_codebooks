Written 2026-09-07, after orientation in the bundle (CLAUDE.md, CONTRIBUTING §4, the characteristic vocabulary, the entity-shielding and owner-shielding views) and after the Getzler/Macnair independence count, but **before** reading Televantos 2020 chapters 2 §2 and 3–6 and before reading Getzler & Macnair 2005 beyond its title. Quoted verbatim in answer 3 of the deliverable.

## What I expect each of the five cells to be

- **AP1 (entity creditors prioritized over members' personal creditors in entity assets) — I expect `1`, confidence medium.** Reasoning I hold going in: the partnership bankruptcy rule of the period (joint estate to joint creditors, separate estate to separate creditors — the 'jingle rule') gives exactly this priority, deed-of-settlement companies were assimilated to partnership, and the trustees' legal title in the joint stock should reinforce it. This is the cell I expect to be best evidenced.
- **AP2 (members *and their creditors* cannot force partition or withdrawal) — I expect `1`, confidence low.** The whole drafting point of a deed of settlement was to exclude the default partnership rules, above all dissolution at will and the death/retirement dissolution. I expect the members' limb to be richly evidenced. I flag in advance that the *creditor* limb is the column's increment over CI2 and that I may find it unevidenced; if so the honest value is `.NR`, not `P`.
- **AP4 (corpus leaves the founder's personal patrimony on constitution) — I expect `.NA`.** The form arises by subscription under a private deed, not by endowment out of an identifiable founder's patrimony, and the vocabulary says where there is no founder the cell is `.NA`, not `0`. I expect to confirm the no-founder fact from the sources rather than assume it.
- **AP3 (members' personal assets shielded from the venture's own creditors beyond contribution) — I expect `0`, confidence medium.** The standard story is that unincorporated companies carried unlimited shareholder liability until 1855, and that this was the price of avoiding incorporation.
- **LR1 (extent and form of members' liability to third parties) — I expect `unlimited-joint`.** Partnership liability was joint, and assimilation to partnership should carry that across.

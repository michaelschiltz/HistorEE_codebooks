## 2. Act 156: a print discrepancy, not an OCR correction

`CORRECTIONS-venetian-ocr-2026-11-05` gives act 156 (Vol. I, PDF 187, p. 154) the rule `OCR-DIGIT+LATIN` and says the regest's third digit is damaged. `REGEST-YEARS` gives the printed regest as "116? (damaged)". The page image reads **«Costantinopoli, 11?9 marzo»**, third digit damaged and most likely a 6 (1169). The other evidence:

- Latin: «millesimo centesimo sexagesimo secundo, mense marcii, inditione decima» (1162).
- Endorsement (Att. XVII): «1172 marzo».
- Neighbours: the editors print the act between nos. 155 and 158, both 1162.

So the editors' regest does not give 1162; it disagrees with their own text. The census rule (the year in the editors' regest) would give 1169; the census of 2026-11-05 records 1162.

**For MS: which year does act 156 take?** **Recommendation: 1162.** The Latin year and the indiction agree, and the editors' own chronological order places the act in 1162, so the regest numeral is a misprint. The rule becomes `PRINT-LATIN`, not `OCR-DIGIT+LATIN`. The period (1150-1199) is the same under either reading, so no table in the selection note moves.

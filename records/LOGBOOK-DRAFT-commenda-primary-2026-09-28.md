## 2026-09-28 — commenda and societas maris re-coded blind from primary acts (pass `commenda-primary-2026-09-28`, stage 4)

**What was done.** The 61 cells of `commenda` and `societas_maris` (organizational census) and `commenda_alloc` and `societas_maris` (loss census) were re-coded blind by `claude-opus-5-5` at effort `high`, from three primary corpora only: Giovanni Scriba vol. I (Genoa 1154–61), Guglielmo Cassinese vol. I (Genoa 1190–91) and Giraud Amalric's cartulary of 1248 as printed by Blancard (Marseille, printed notulae only). The priors file was committed before any source was opened (sha256 `5fcf23fc…d182c`).

**Method.** Every act was typed by its Latin drafting term (2,459 printed acts; 470 Amalric stubs counted apart). The commenda and societas strata of each corpus were taken whole where 60 acts or fewer, otherwise sampled systematically (seed 20260928): 272 instances from 268 acts. Every sampled act was read in the Latin and its parties, clauses and characteristic states recorded with verbatim quotes; the quotes that carry minority states in the Genoese acts (51 instances) were checked against the page images. Each cell takes the modal state among the acts that speak to it, pooled; states held by at least 10% of the speaking acts in any corpus are named as variants; `.NR` where no act speaks.

**Result.** 36 observed cells, 21 `.NR`, 4 `.NA`. Fifteen of my priors were falsified, among them `LR3` (`P`, not `1`, for both rows: a fixed fraction of profit that does not track the stake), `societas_maris CF2` (`1`, low, carried by land-based societates owing the capital at a term; `0` among sea-going acts) and `commenda_alloc RB3` (`surety`; every surety in the sample answers for the working party's fault, not for the peril). Cassinese never drafts a peril clause and Blancard usually abridges it, so every cell that turns on the peril rests on 5 to 22 acts.

**Decisions to adjudicate.** (1) Whether land-based societates belong in the `societas_maris` frame: the frame was fixed by drafting term, and this one decision sets `CF2` and most of the `TS2` variant. (2) Whether a promise to pay back the capital at term is `CF2=1` or a performance duty. (3) Whether `PR1=0` is observed where a peril clause states no charge. All decision points, with their alternatives, are in the coding notes.

**Flagged, not repaired.** `TS2` cannot hold a multi-voyage horizon; `RB3` cannot say that a surety covers fault only; `LR3` is undefined for the gratuitous commenda; `CI1` cannot hold a common stock with side placements kept separately; the `societas_maris` row does not correspond to the drafting term `societas`, which also covers land partnerships; no Venetian act was in the sources although the row names the *collegantia*.

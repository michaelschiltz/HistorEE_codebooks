Blind primary-source re-coding of commenda and societas maris (stage 4)

Re-codes the 61 cells of commenda and societas_maris (organizational
census) and commenda_alloc and societas_maris (loss census) blind, from
Giovanni Scriba I, Guglielmo Cassinese I and Amalric 1248 (Blancard,
printed notulae only), with no secondary literature.

Why: to test the live values against what the notarial acts themselves
say, with priors committed first and the live values withheld.

- Type census of 2,459 printed acts by Latin drafting term.
- Systematic sample (seed 20260928): 272 instances from 268 acts,
  every party and clause recorded with verbatim Latin; minority-state
  quotes in the Genoese acts checked against page images.
- Cell = modal state among the acts that speak, pooled; variants of
  10% or more in any corpus named; .NR where no act speaks.
- 36 observed cells, 21 .NR, 4 .NA; fifteen priors falsified.
- Both recodings files validate (frictionless 5.19.1, foreign key
  removed on scratch copies).

Main open decision: land-based societates sit in the societas_maris
frame because the frame was fixed by drafting term; they carry
societas_maris CF2=1 (low). Sea-going acts alone give CF2=0.

## 8. D1 — the `LR3` definition (worksheet entries 3 and 6)

`LR3` is clarified on existing usage, value names kept: the test is a share in the outcome against a fixed claim; "proportionally to stake" is a gloss in which labour counts as a stake, so a negotiated ratio that does not follow capital is `1`; `P` is reserved for an arrangement partly fixed and partly shared. ## 9. D2 — a promise to render the capital is not an assumption of peril (`CF2`)

An evidence rule is added to `CF2`'s definition: a promise to render, restore or bring back the capital, with or without a term or penalty, is a duty of performance and not by itself evidence for `CF2`; `1` or `P` requires that the act or the law place the loss of the principal's capital on the working party in terms, or keep the obligation alive on total loss. Read first-hand: Amalric 429 (Blancard II, 35–36; page images), where "et tuum resegum et meum" and "totum capitale … reducere" stand in one act; Scriba CCCLV (1158, 185; "capitale tuum super me salvum erit"), an explicit assumption; Scriba DCLXXIV (1160, 364), a father's guarantee, recorded as not the working party's exposure. Worksheet entry 18 is ruled after D4.

## 10. D3 — what `LR4` counts

`LR4` is clarified on existing usage, value names kept: it counts the pooling of members' separate, member-specific outcomes or hazards, so that one member's own loss is borne in part by the others. Dividing one common venture's outcome by shares is `LR3`'s question and is `0` here; so is a clause sending a member's earnings on a separate stake into the common stock (Scriba CLXXXI, 1157, p. 96, read in context: the investor's extra £31, *quarum totum proficuum debet esse suum preter quartam que debet reverti in societatem ipsam*). An agreement pooling the profits of several separate ventures counts, on the arrangement it creates: Amalric 697 (1248; Blancard II, 153–154, read in context), three Jewish travellers to Valencia on the *Leopardus* making the profit of *omnium comandarum … sint eis communes equis porcionibus*, own money taking profit *pro solido & libra*. Ground: of 28 `LR4` cells, the 17 `0`s include every common-stock venture in the census (the three VOC rows, both *maone*, `partenrederij`, `bazacle_mill`, `casa_san_giorgio`, `chartered_corporation_england`), each coded `0` in its notes on one common exposure with nothing member-specific pooled; three of the four `1`s (`nakai_fictive_household`, across stores; `begijnhof`; `bruderschaft_salzburg`) rest on pooling members' separate fortunes. **The fourth, `asiento_averia` (`OF-0110`), is coded `1` "for the vehicle", whose *participes* "pool capital and share loss proportionally" — the ground this clarification excludes; its note says the levy limb would also carry `1`, "but the members differ". Found while applying, not raised when D3 was put; flagged below, not repaired.** The strongest objection — that this infers `0` from form rather than evidence — is answered by that house practice; whether commingling (`CI5`) makes a venture pool is left open (logbook 4, 2026-10-12 (iii)). No value moves by the clarification itself; entries 4, 5, 7 and 17 are ruled separately. No version bump: a definition, not a declared constraint.

## 13. D4 — acts are assigned to the Italian rows by structure, not by drafting term and not by terrain

Arm P fixed its frames by drafting term (*accomendatio* → `commenda`, *societas* → `societas_maris`) and proposed, as the alternative, restricting them to voyages. Neither is the census's criterion. Acts are assigned by structure: **`commenda`** when the working party trades capital he did not contribute for a share of profit; **`societas_maris`** when both contribute and the working party trades the joint stock (van Doosselaere 2009, 65, the type rows' own distinction). **Sea and land alike**, travelling or not, as the comanda was split on 2026-10-07 on the loss rule and not on terrain (Fynn-Paul 2017, 91: the land–sea distinction not a contemporary one); Pisa's *Constitutum usus* puts the *societas maris vel terre* under one rule (Bonaini II, 839 n. 2). **Out**, on structure as the comanda deposit was on 2026-10-11: Cassinese 875 (a deposit returnable fifteen days after demand, no profit), Cassinese 362 (goods held *nomine pignoris*), and Amalric 697 (another contract, ruling 12). Stationary land uses — shops, workshops, an exchange table — stay in; where they differ (`TS2` fixed-term), the difference is a variant by count. The re-sort uses only fields arm P recorded at stage 4 (`capital_tractator`, destination, notes): 146 `commenda` acts, 123 `societas_maris` acts, 3 out; 18 acts drafted as *societas* are unilateral and move to `commenda`. Codes and names kept; scope written on both type rows. **Disclosed before ruling:** the frame decides one cell, `commenda CI2` (entry 10: two land acts under this ruling, none under a voyage restriction); on `societas_maris CF2` (entry 18) both frames give the same answer. ## 20. Ruling 15 reopened — `CI2 = 1` for `commenda` and `societas_maris` (worksheet entries 10 and 14)

Ruling 15 kept `.NR` because recall of delivered capital was a default-law question with no rule read. Pisa's *Constitutum usus* has the rule (Bonaini II, 886, page image; rubric XXII, covering the *societas* and the contribution *ad portandum in compagniam*): where the investor gave power to go *in quodcumque tassedium* and later orders the traveller home, *usque ad completum primum tassedium … redire vel remittere non teneatur*; once it is complete he must return or remit; where the place was named, on completing the named venture. The apparatus caps the whole at five ([M.]) or three ([Io.]) years without the investor's express will. By default, then, the investor cannot recall the capital during a venture, and for the single-venture contract — the mode on both rows — the bar covers the arrangement's whole life. `CI2` `.NR` → `1`, `medium` (one city's law, the `AP1` standard), `articulated` (the statute states the bar; this is not the inference from term that the `hegu_yingu` precedent declined). The at-will acts are drafted exceptions to the default, recorded by count under ruling 2; Scriba DXXVI's express power to divide at will through a messenger is a clause one drafts only against such a default. The open mandate — recall possible between ventures — would be the `voc_1602` pattern (`P`) and is the minority form. The objection that `voc_1602` codes "locked for a term, then withdrawable" as `P` is answered by the form: `voc_1602` is a continuing company with an exit at its ten-year account, while here the bar covers the contract's life, as for `asiento_averia` (`1`, "immobilised for the fixed term"). `comanda CI2` stays `.NR`; the family differs on `CI2` by evidence, not substance. New rows `OF-R0210`, `OF-R0211` **replaced**; the six pilot rows move from **kept** to **overruled**.

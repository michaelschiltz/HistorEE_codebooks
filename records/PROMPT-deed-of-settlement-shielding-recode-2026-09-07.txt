there. `deed_of_settlement_company` is the English UNINCORPORATED joint-stock
company constituted by deed of settlement, c.1720-1844: a partnership whose

settled legal title to the circulating trade assets on a fixed body of
trustees, with control divided between directors, a general court of

settlement, a private deed — no Royal Charter, no letters patent, no Act.

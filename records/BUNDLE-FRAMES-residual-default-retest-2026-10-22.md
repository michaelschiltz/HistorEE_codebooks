# Reading the sources

Two items in the National Diet Library Digital Collections. You read **nineteen** rulebooks in them, and the forms printed with them, and nothing else.

## Source S: 司法省調査部『世態調査資料』第三十六號 (c. 1942), pid 1909099

**Transmission service (送信サービス)**: it opens only in the maintainer's Chrome, where he is logged in to NDL. The IIIF image service refuses this item; read it in the **viewer only**, `https://dl.ndl.go.jp/pid/1909099/1/<frame>`, and use the viewer's own zoom, or Chrome's zoom on a region, for detail. ## Also open to you

The title frame and the table of contents of each item (the viewer's 目次 tab). Nothing else.

## What you must not read

- **Anything in either item outside the pages above**: not the Finance Ministry's chapters on 無盡講 (pp. 239–256), not the Aomori or Tokushima reports' text, round-table discussions or regulations, and not the Okinawa report on 模合.
- **Any other NDL item, any other website, and any other source on kō.** No search, no bibliographic record beyond the two items' own title frames and tables of contents.
- If you read part of a page outside the list by accident, say what and where in your notes, and do not use it.

## What the sources tell you, and what they do not

Neither source prints a commentary on each rulebook. **Code from the clauses and the forms.** Record each rulebook's place (村, 町 or 市, and 郡 and 縣 where printed) as printed in its heading, text or signatures, or write `not printed`. Personal names may be printed in full; they are not evidence of anything and are not to be repeated in your notes.

Source reference only, after the precedent of `LM-0545` (2026-10-11 (v)): no value, confidence or articulation moves, so no recodings row and no version bump; the twelve recodings rows of 2026-10-12 keep "(1862)" as the record of what that pass wrote. The Datta fragment was issued in the volume's second fascicle (Rendiconto, p. 642), which cannot be dated from the copy held: no earlier than 1859, since Desimoni's report cites Datta's Nice edition of 1859 as *testé* published (p. 93 n.), and no later than the Rendiconto's approval on 9 March 1862. Pryor 1977, n. 13, gives "parte 2 (1859)" without a source. The cells cite the volume, not the fascicle; the fascicle evidence is in logbook 5.

Promis, *Statuti della colonia genovese di Pera*, stays "(1871)": the title page of *Miscellanea di storia italiana* XI, read at 400 dpi, prints MDCCCLXXI, and the pencilled "1871" beside it agrees. The flag of logbook 5, 2026-10-12 (viii), that the printed date is 1870, rested on a misreading of the imprint at low resolution; Zotero `CTM27ZV8` is returned to 1871. ## 4. "…Scriba 1.pdf" is the 1956 inventory of the Genoese notarial cartularies: kept, given its own record, OCR'd

The 139-page file linked to `QTEG4PM7` as "Chiaudano and Moresco - 1935 - Il cartolare di Giovanni Scriba 1.pdf" is not Scriba. Read on the page images, it is Archivio di Stato di Genova, *Cartolari notarili genovesi (1–149). Inventario*, vol. I, parte prima (Pubblicazioni degli Archivi di Stato XXII, Roma, Ministero dell'Interno, 1956), to p. 251: the Società Ligure di Storia Patria library copy, scanned as two-page spreads without a text layer, with a handwritten note that the errata are entered by hand in the text. It inventories each cartulary by folio range, date range and notary; cartolare 1 is Giovanni Scriba's, and p. 2 notes agreement with the Chiaudano–Moresco edition on the fragments. Kept as the finding aid for the Genoese notarial corpus: new Zotero record `JPEAPSEZ`; the spreads split into 278 pages, OCR'd in Italian (tesseract 5, `ita` best model, page-segmentation mode 4, which keeps a row's folio range with its notary), images recompressed; the OCR copy linked as `V7L6S5S9`. The image-only scan's attachment (`9PW8MDU5`, re-parented to `JPEAPSEZ` first) is trashed and its file moved to `ZoteroFiles/_to_delete/`. The text layer locates; it is not to be quoted (digits are misread, e.g. "18 v." for 13 v.).

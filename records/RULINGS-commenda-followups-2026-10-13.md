## 6. `CF3 = bilateral` → `CI1 = none` is not registered as a dependence

Neither relation the register knows fits. `applicability_on` gates a cell to `.NA`, and `CI1 = none` is a substantive value; a `dependence_group` would be falsified on registration, because `isqa` (`CF1 = 1`, `CF3 = bilateral`) is `CI1 = several-accounts` under the definition's "ownership halves" clause, and `societas_maris` (bilateral, `CF1 = P`) is `common`. `CI1`, `CF3` and `CF1` are all component `none`, so no comparative claim is exposed to double counting; and a registered group would invite reading the four principal–agent `.NR` cells (`comanda`, `comanda_ad_societatem`, `ortoq_equity`, `qirad`) as `none`, against ruling 4 of 2026-10-11. The near-entailment is recorded in logbook 4, with the census cross-tab; to be revisited if `CI1` or `CF3` enters a component set.

## 7. `AP1` counts only third parties who dealt with the arrangement

`AP1` ("entity creditors prioritized over members' personal creditors in entity assets") is clarified on the usage of eight of its nine substantive cells: entity creditors are third parties who dealt with the arrangement, the class `AP3` calls the venture's own creditors. A member's claim on the pool, residual or for capital invested, is not an entity creditor's claim; the commenda investor's priority over the traveller's creditors is a partition in a member's favour, carried in the cell note. Applied: `commenda` and `societas_maris` `AP1` `P` → `0`, medium, analyst-imposed (`OF-R0220`, `R0221`): the Genoese Breve c. VI, re-read on the page images, ranks the accomendator or socius first in movables ("preferatur aliis creditoribus. et uxori et nurui") and every other creditor of the traveller alike, so the venture's creditors are not set before his personal creditors; one chapter, one datum. `comanda` `P` → `.NR` (`OF-R0222`): *Recognoverunt proceres* c. 69, held only in Martínez Gijón's paraphrase (421, re-read on the page image), voids the claims of "cualquier acreedor del comendatario" until the investors recover, which would read `0` as Genoa does if the paraphrase is faithful; the statute's text is not held. `isqa` `0` → `.NR` (`OF-R0223`): its `0` answered the investor's insulation by bailment. Definition extended in `vocabularies/organizational_form_characteristic.csv`. Count at clarification, 26 cells: `1` ×8, `P` ×1 (`bazacle_mill`), `0` ×2, `.NR` ×15. The vault note "Barcelona law kept the commendatary's creditors off the comanda goods" and its MOC entry corrected.

## 9. The travellers' pool of Amalric 697 stays parked: one instance in the held corpora

MS asked for a recurrence search before deciding; nothing was coded. A pact among travellers to pool the profits of commendas each had received separately from outside investors is attested once: Amalric 697 (Marseille, 11 May 1248; Blancard II, 153–154; Pryor 1981, notula 84, whose edition restores "[et que facient]", commendas still to be received). Pryor 1984, 436, reading the whole cartulary, calls it an exception to tractators' keeping their affairs to themselves. Nothing of the type in Blancard I–II otherwise, Giovanni Scriba I, Guglielmo Cassinese I, fourteen Crown of Aragon files, or the held Geniza and Jewish-law literature (Goitein, *A Mediterranean Society* I, *India Traders*, *Letters*, *Studies*; Ackerman-Lieberman, Cohen, Gil, Stillman, Goldberg, Khan, Udovitch, Bondioli, Gamoran, Khalilieh). Neighbours are attested throughout and are other contracts: one commenda held by several agents (Pryor 1984, 436 n. 54; Goitein, *Letters*, 271 n. 1); co-travellers' partnerships of their own capital (Blancard II 486; Cassinese 379; *Med. Soc.* I, 176); the *khulṭa*, pooled own capital; and clauses reverting a traveller's share on goods carried outside a *societas* into it (Scriba CCLXXV, like CLXXXI). MS's ground: an arrangement in common use would be at least mentioned in the held literature. Not held: the Hebrew *India Book* I–IV and *A Mediterranean Society* II–VI.
